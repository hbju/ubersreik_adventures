### Training

Train in a Skill or Characteristic **outside your current Career**, treating it as in-career for advancement purposes.

In addition to the normal **XP cost**, pay a tutor fee:
- **Basic Skills and all Characteristics:** XP cost + **1d10 brass pennies**.
- **Advanced Skills:** Double the above amount.

Tutors for most skills are available in large cities. Unusual skills may require a **[Consult an Expert](/codex/endeavours/consult-an-expert)** Endeavour to locate an instructor. Proscribed knowledge (Ruinous Powers, Necromancy, etc.) requires extraordinary effort to source and will almost certainly demand a **[Significant Favour](/codex/endeavours/do-me-a-favour!)** in addition to inflated fees.
