# Altdorf Endeavours

The following Endeavours may be undertaken by Characters in **Altdorf** (or adapted for similar major cities), in addition to those in the Core Rulebook.

---

## Penance

Walk one of Altdorf's holy paths in the footsteps of Sigmar to reduce accumulated **Sin points**.

Make an **Average (+20) Pray Test**:

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Success | Remove **1 Sin point**. |
| Impressive Success (+4 SL) | Remove **2 Sin points**. |
| Failure | Suffer 1 **Fatigued** Condition for the first day of the next adventure. |
| Fumble | Roll for the **Wrath of the Gods** *(WFRP p.218)*. |

---

## Train with Unusual Weapon

Master a weapon so unusual that tutors can only help so far — true mastery requires personal trial and error.

Make a **Difficult (–10) Melee or Ranged Test**, matching the specialisation to the weapon you are training with.

- **Success:** You have mastered the weapon.
- **Failure:** You must wait until the next period of downtime before attempting again.

---

## Test Magic Item

Research the properties of a magical artefact using the **Research** Skill. Only characters with the Research Skill may attempt this.

Make a **Difficult (–10) Research Test**:

| SL | Outcome | Information Learned |
|---|---|---|
| +6 or more | Astounding Success | Full potential of the artefact — how to use it, all quirks and dangers. |
| +4 to +5 | Impressive Success | *(same as above)* |
| +2 to +3 | Success | Primary purpose and how to activate it. |
| +0 to +1 | Marginal Success | *(same as above)* |
| –0 to –1 | Marginal Failure | Nothing useful learned. |
| –2 to –3 | Failure | *(same as above)* |
| –4 to –5 | Impressive Failure | Nothing learned + test for **Moderate Exposure to Corruption**. |
| –6 or less | Astounding Failure | *(same as above)* |

---

## Patronage

*Requires: also taking the **Banking** Endeavour in Altdorf.*

Invest at least **5 GC** with an up-and-coming playwright as a patron of the arts. This Endeavour must be taken **twice** — once to invest, once to recover.

When recovering, make a **Challenging (+0) Evaluate Test**:

| SL | Outcome |
|---|---|
| +6 or more | Instant classic. Reverse any Fellowship Tests with theatre-lovers next adventure. Investment returned **+20%**. |
| +3 to +5 | Moderate success. Reverse **one** Fellowship Test with arts-lovers next adventure. Investment returned. |
| +0 to +2 | Somewhat pedantic but tickets sold. Reverse **one** Fellowship Test. **Half** investment returned. |
| –0 to –2 | Extremely tedious. Investment lost. |
| –3 to –5 | Offensively bad. Investment lost. An offended noble **swears to duel everyone** who sponsored the play. |
| –6 or worse | Disaster — tax evasion scheme or Chaotic ritual disguised as a play. Attracts attention of either **Altdorf's Royal Tax Inspector** or a **Witch Hunter** (your choice). |
