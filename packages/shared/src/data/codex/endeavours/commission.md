### Commission

Acquire unusual, specialised, or unique items that are never simply stocked in shops.

If you know a source capable of acquiring or manufacturing an **Exotic** rarity item, spend the funds and place your order during this Endeavour. The item will be ready after your next adventure. Only **one Exotic item** may be commissioned per Endeavour.

If you do not know an appropriate source, you must first complete a **[Consult an Expert](/codex/endeavours/consult-an-expert)** Endeavour.

> **Custom Gear:** With GM permission, two or more consecutive **Commission** Endeavours may be used to acquire truly extraordinary items — personal engineering commissions, rare poisons, or magically ensorcelled gear. Costs range from very high to staggering and may require **Favours** or a dedicated adventure to obtain raw materials.
