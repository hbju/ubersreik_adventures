### Banking

Store funds for future use. All money not saved through this Endeavour is lost at the end of the between-adventures phase (see **[Money to Burn](/codex/endeavours/endeavours)**).

Choose one of two methods:

**Investing**
Available to characters of the **Gold or Silver** social tier only. Money deposited in a banking house will accrue interest.

When depositing, choose (or roll **1d10**) an **interest rate** between 1 and 10. This represents both your return and the risk of failure. When withdrawing (requires another **Banking** Endeavour), roll **1d100**:
- Roll **equal to or less than** your interest rate: the bank has gone bankrupt — all money is lost.
- Roll **over** the interest rate: receive your initial deposit plus the accrued interest.

**Stashing**
Available to all characters. Hide your money on your person, under a mattress, or buried somewhere.

Stashed money accrues no interest. Withdrawing requires **no Endeavour** — simply roll **1d100** before the next adventure:
- Roll **10 or lower**: your stash has been discovered — all money is lost.
- Roll **11+**: recover your full stash.

Successfully recovered stash funds are available at the start of the next adventure, in addition to any **Income** Endeavour funds.

> **Example:** Gerhard deposits 10 GC with the banking house Bent, Crooke & Scarper. The GM rolls a **6** — Gerhard earns 6% interest, but must roll above 6 on **1d100** when withdrawing or lose everything. Ulli buries his 10 GC by a mile-marker. No Endeavour needed to retrieve it — but he rolls a **07**, finds only disturbed earth and an empty chest, and reflects on his life choices.
