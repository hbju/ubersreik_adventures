### Foment Dissent
*Burghers, Peasants*

Sow civil unrest by agitating locals against a specific individual, group, or institution. This Endeavour takes **2 Endeavours** to complete (and counts as an **Income** Endeavour for characters in the Agitator career).

**Step 1:** Attempt an **Average (+20) [Gossip](/codex/skills/gossip)** Test to understand local politics.

**Step 2:** If successful, attempt a **[Charm](/codex/skills/charm)** Test with Difficulty determined by how unpopular the target is:
- Rousing anger against an oppressive noble: **Easy (+40)**.
- Rallying a mob against a Shallyan hospice: **Hard (–20)**.

Failure at either step ends the Endeavour. Success means that during your next adventure you may attempt a **[Charm](/codex/skills/charm)** Test to gather an angry mob against your chosen target. The degree of success determines the crowd's ferocity — an Astounding Success may produce an uncontrollable lynch mob. Failure (especially severe) may expose your activities to the target or their sympathisers.

Once a mob has been rallied, redirecting them against a different target is possible but harder — **two Difficulty levels higher**.
