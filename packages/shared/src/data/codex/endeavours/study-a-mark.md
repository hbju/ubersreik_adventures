### Study a Mark
*Rogues*

Observe a target to gain an advantage in future criminal enterprises — learning a merchant's habits for an impersonation, mapping a temple's routines for a robbery.

Attempt a **Challenging (+0) [Perception](/codex/skills/perception)** Test (Difficulty may be modified for especially easy or difficult subjects). On success, you may **reverse one Test** concerning your mark once during your next adventure (note this on your character sheet). This Endeavour may be undertaken multiple times.

The GM should also provide information — or deliberate disinformation — about the mark according to the SL scored.
