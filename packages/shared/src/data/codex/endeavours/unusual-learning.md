### Unusual Learning

Attempt to learn a **Talent outside your current Career**. Success is not guaranteed — you may spend XP and money and gain nothing.

You must first locate an appropriate tutor (a **[Consult an Expert](/codex/endeavours/consult-an-expert)** Endeavour may be required for obscure Talents).

Once a tutor is found, pay a fee of **2d10 shillings per 100 XP** the Talent costs. Then attempt a **Hard (–20) Test** using the most relevant Characteristic or Skill, as determined by the GM:
- **Success:** You learn the Talent.
- **Failure:** You do not learn it this time, but gain **+10** to future attempts for each prior failure.
