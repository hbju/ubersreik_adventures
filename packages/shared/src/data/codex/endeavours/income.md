### Income

This Endeavour covers how you earn money between adventures — working your trade, collecting stipends, pursuing bounties, or more roguish means of acquisition.

Briefly narrate how your character makes money (this is flavour, though GMs may find useful hooks in the details). You then receive **standard income** based on your **Status**.

Certain Events Table results may modify Income earnings. Money from this Endeavour is received after the last adventure's funds are spent.

Additionally, characters in **Career tiers 3 or 4** who complete this Endeavour automatically **maintain their standing** in their career (see **[With Great Power…](/codex/endeavours/duties-and-responsabilities)**).
