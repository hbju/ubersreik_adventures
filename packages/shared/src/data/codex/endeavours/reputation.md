### Reputation
*Academics, Burghers, Courtiers*

Spend money to temporarily elevate your **Standing** by **+1** for the next adventure (after which the boost fades). The cost is equal to your **maximum Earning income** (see Status rules).

Attempt a **Challenging (+0) Career Skill** Test:
- **Success:** Standing increases by +1.
- **Astounding Success (+6 SL):** Standing increases by +2.
- **Failure:** Money wasted; no benefit.
- **Astounding Failure (–6 SL):** Your toadying irritates your peers; Standing **decreases by –1** (minimum 0) for the entire next adventure.
