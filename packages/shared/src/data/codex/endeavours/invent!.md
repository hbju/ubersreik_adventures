### Invent!

Create entirely new trappings by combining existing ones (Pistol + Pistol = Repeating Pistol; Pigeon + Bomb = Pigeon Bomb).

**Stage 1 — Plan**
Spend 1 Endeavour and attempt a **[Trade (Engineer)](/codex/skills/trade)** Test, with Difficulty set by the GM based on how outlandish the combination is. On success, your blueprints are ready; each SL scored adds **+1 SL** to the Building attempt.

**Stage 2 — Build**
Use either a **[Crafting](/codex/endeavours/crafting)** or **[Commission](/codex/endeavours/commission)** Endeavour:

- **Crafting:** You must possess all relevant Skills for the combined trappings. Difficulty is always **Very Hard (–30)**. Raw materials cost **twice** the listed price for the trappings being combined.
- **Commission:** Complete a **[Consult an Expert](/codex/endeavours/consult-an-expert)** Endeavour first to find a capable craftsperson, then follow the **[Commission](/codex/endeavours/commission)** Endeavour rules. Commissioning costs **six times** the listed price for the combined trappings.

Final rules for the new invention are determined by the GM.
