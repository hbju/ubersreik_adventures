# Magic Endeavours

The following Endeavours are available to spellcasting characters between adventures.

---

## Brew Potion

Attempt to brew a single batch of a particular potion following the standard brewing rules. Assumes you have all ingredients to hand (or have taken the **Gather Ingredients** Endeavour below).

Requires access to a **laboratory**, unless you have the **Concoct** Talent.

---

## Gather Ingredients

Spend one Endeavour gathering ingredients for potions in an appropriate locale (as described in the magic rules). If you fail on the first attempt, you may try again as a second Endeavour.

---

## Improve Familiar

Your familiar cannot improve itself — you must nurture it. Make a **Hard (–20) Research Test** (the Familiar may assist). On a success, the Familiar may immediately undertake **one** of the following Endeavours:

- Training *(WFRP p.199)*
- Unusual Learning *(WFRP p.199 — may not learn Bless, Invoke, or Pure Soul)*
- Train with Unusual Weapon *(Combat Familiars only)*
- Test Magic Item *(Spell Familiars only)*

---

## Perform Ritual

Rituals can be performed as Endeavours. Benefits over performing during play:

- You can find a site of **Heavy or Extreme** magical Saturation.
- The ritual's **CN is halved** (rounding up), reflecting the care taken — though the same total magical energy must still be channelled.

**Drawbacks:** Effects may have worn off by the time the adventure begins. Enemies may learn of the working and prepare countermeasures.

---

## College Research

*Available to licensed wizards at their alma mater.*

Make a **Challenging (+0) Research Test**. Each +SL allows you to **memorise one spell for 100 XP less** than normal (minimum 100 XP). You must purchase any reduced-cost spells **immediately**.

This Endeavour has no benefit for wizards who have not yet memorised a number of spells equal to their Intelligence Bonus.
