### The Latest News
*Rangers, Riverfolk*

Gather intelligence from travellers, traders, and rumour-mongers. Attempt a **Challenging (+0) [Gossip](/codex/skills/gossip)** Test. On success, learn one interesting rumour; each additional SL reveals another (they may connect to the next adventure). On an **Impressive Failure**, you learn something convincingly false — the GM may make this roll in secret.
