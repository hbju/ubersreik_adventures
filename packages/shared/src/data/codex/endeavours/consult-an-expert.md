### Consult an Expert

Seek out an authority on a specific subject to facilitate another Endeavour (**[Commission](/codex/endeavours/commission)**, **[Training](/codex/endeavours/training)**, **[Unusual Learning](/codex/endeavours/unusual-learning)**) or to acquire a specific piece of knowledge.

**Stage 1 — Locate the Expert**
This stage may be skipped if an appropriate expert was already identified during play. Otherwise, attempt a **Challenging (+0) [Gossip](/codex/skills/gossip)** Test (Difficulty varies by settlement size). On success, you locate your expert. On failure, you locate the loudest self-proclaimed specialist in the area — their advice may or may not be reliable.

**Stage 2 — Consult**
Convince the expert to share their knowledge. Depending on what you need, this may require a simple **[Charm](/codex/skills/charm)** Test, a donation to the local Temple of Verena, or the promise of one or more **Favours** (see **[Do Me a Favour!](/codex/endeavours/do-me-a-favour!)**). The complexity and danger of the information determines the level of Favour required.

On a successful consult for specific knowledge (not used to facilitate another Endeavour), you also gain an **Expert Reroll** (note on your character sheet) usable on any Test relating to the revealed lore before the end of your next adventure.
