## Duties & Responsibilities

### With Great Power…

> 'The common folk will never understand my burdens. The sheer weight of the duties I bear. The guilty will not judge themselves, and there are always so many villages to cleanse, for our enemies are legion.'
> — Lothar Metzger, Witch Hunter

Characters who have reached the **top two tiers of their Career Path** accrue responsibilities that cannot be neglected. If such a character does **not** undertake the **[Income](/codex/endeavours/income)** Endeavour, they **drop one level** within their Career (e.g., from fourth to third) at no XP cost. This reduces their Status and future Income Endeavour earnings. Advances acquired at the lost level are retained. Reclaiming the lost level costs the normal XP to re-enter it.

### Elf Improvement

Between adventures, all **Elven characters** must spend **one Endeavour** maintaining contact with their own kind — filing reports with agents of Ulthuan (High Elves) or with spies from their arboreal homes (Wood Elves). This Endeavour provides no mechanical benefit beyond being an Elf.

> **Note:** Elves only lose an Endeavour to this obligation if the between-adventures period is **at least 3 weeks long** (meaning there are at least 3 Endeavours available).
