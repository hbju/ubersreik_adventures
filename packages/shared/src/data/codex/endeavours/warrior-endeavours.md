# Warrior Endeavours

Warrior Endeavours refine martial skill. **Any character** may attempt them, but those who have **never belonged to the Warrior Class** find all Tests one Difficulty Level harder (e.g. Difficult becomes Hard).

---

## Fanmaris's Perfect Shot

Study Allavandrel Fanmaris's training manual and practice under distracting conditions.

Make a **Difficult (–10) Ranged (Bow) Test**.

On a **success**, unlock the following benefit once during the next adventure: after spending one Round in combat **aiming** at your opponent, declare a **Perfect Shot**. Make your Ranged Test normally (including the +20 for aiming). Gains additional effects based on total SL:

| SL | Effect (cumulative) |
|---|---|
| +1 | Choose the hit location. |
| +2–3 | Weapon gains the **Penetrating** Quality for this attack (or +2 Damage if already Penetrating). |
| +4–5 | Deliver an automatic **Critical Hit**. |
| +6 | Weapon gains the **Damaging** Quality for this attack (or gains **Impact** if already Damaging). |

*You may undertake this Endeavour multiple times.*

## The Leitdorf Defence

Study Count Marius Leitdorf's pamphlet on unconventional offensive manoeuvres — disorienting tactics designed to bypass orthodox defences at considerable personal risk.

Make a **Difficult (–10) Melee Test**.

On a **success**, unlock the following benefit once during the next adventure: after one or more rounds engaged with an opponent, declare the **Leitdorf Defence**. Make an **Opposed Melee Test at –10**.

- Your opponent **may not use their Talents or Skill Advances** when defending.
- If **you win:** Suffer 1d10 Damage ignoring Armour.
- If **your opponent wins:** They inflict a **Critical Hit** — the risky manoeuvre leaves you exposed.

*You may undertake this Endeavour multiple times.*

## Alcatini Method

*Requires: at least two ranks of the **Drilled** Talent.*

Use a mixture of shouting, threats, speeches, alcohol, and bribery to rapidly forge a group of miscreants into a functioning unit. These lessons fade without regular practice.

Make a **Difficult (–10) Leadership Test**. For each SL, grant one character **+1 rank of Drilled** for the duration of the next adventure.

- Characters **already with Drilled** may increase their rank by 1, up to the endeavouring character's own Drilled rank.
- Characters with the **Inspiring** Talent multiply the number of characters affected, as per the standard Inspiring table.

> **Example:** +3 SL with 2 ranks of Inspiring could grant +1 Drilled to up to 30 characters.

## Count Punchausen's Narrative Auction

Sell your tale to a pfennig dreadful printer for coin and notoriety.

Make a **Difficult (–10) Charm Test** or a **Challenging (+0) Entertain (Storytelling) Test**.

On a **success:** Receive **2d10 shillings** and, once during the next adventure, you may **reverse the dice** on a Charm or Entertain (Storytelling) Test — someone in the audience has heard your tale.

## The Quartermaster Shuffle

Acquire a specific piece of equipment through dangerous and shady means.

1. **Choose a Trapping**, apply any Qualities, and calculate its final cost (rounded up to the nearest GC). Maximum 20 GC unless the GM waives this.
2. Make a **Challenging (+0) Gossip Test** to locate an associate with a suitable job. On a success, you are given details of a job where the item can be acquired as payment or taken during the job.
3. Make a **Difficult (–10) Melee or Ranged Test**:
   - **Success:** Receive the item and a Critical Wound to a random location with a **–20 penalty** to critical severity.
   - **Failure:** Receive the item and a Critical Wound to a random location with a **bonus to severity equal to the item's cost in GC**.

Critical Deflection cannot negate this Critical Wound, but a Fate point might.
