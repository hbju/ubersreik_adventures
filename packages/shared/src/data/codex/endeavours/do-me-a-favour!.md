## Do Me a Favour!

A **Favour** is a future obligation taken on in exchange for immediate assistance. Breaking a sworn Favour is considered deeply dishonourable in the Empire: **Status is reduced by 1**, and powerful creditors may pursue violent consequences.

Favours come in three levels:

**Minor Favour**
A simple task completable in a few hours — volunteering at a mercy-house, putting in a good word for someone. With GM permission, may be paid off by spending **1 Endeavour**.

**Major Favour**
A more time-consuming or risky undertaking — weeks of effort, possible travel, real danger. Perhaps acting as lookout on a burglary, negotiating a guild war, or transporting a package across the Empire. With GM permission, may be paid off by spending **2 or more consecutive Endeavours**.

**Significant Favour**
Rare, dangerous, and impossible to pay off with Endeavours alone. Likely involves months of travel, serious violence, and risking life and limb — eliminating a rival gang boss, wiping out a Goblin encampment, or fomenting a riot. **Significant Favours are always roleplayed as full adventures.**

> Note: Even the most powerful creditors generally tailor their demands to the capabilities and ethics of the person who owes them. No one expects a Priestess of Shallya to commit assassination on their behalf — though Favours are frequently used to exert uncomfortable pressure on a character's moral compass.
