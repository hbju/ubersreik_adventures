### Research Lore
*Academics*

Seek knowledge on a specific subject — a historical event, a location, an individual — using a relevant storehouse of lore (library, Guild records, Temple of Verena, Dwarf Hold annals, etc.).

Attempt an **Average (+20) [Lore](/codex/skills/lore)** Test using the appropriate Lore Specialisation. Without the correct Specialisation, a literate character may substitute a **Difficult (–10) [Intelligence](/codex/characteristics/intelligence)** Test instead.

On success, learn one piece of interesting, useful, or hidden knowledge; each SL reveals an additional detail. On an **Impressive** or **Astounding Failure**, you learn something convincingly false — the GM may make this roll in secret.
