## Combat

When diplomacy fails, combat begins. During combat, time is structured as follows:

- **Round:** Enough time for all combatants to act — roughly a few seconds.
- **Turn:** Each combatant's individual action within a Round.
- **Initiative Order:** Combatants act from **highest to lowest Initiative**. Ties broken by **Agility**; further ties resolved by an **[Opposed](/codex/mechanics/opposed-tests) Agility Test**.

### Combat Summary

1. **Determine Surprise** — GM determines if any characters are [Surprised](/codex/condition/condition_surprised).
2. **Round Begins** — Apply any start-of-round effects.
3. **Characters Take Turns** — In **Initiative** order, each combatant takes a **Move** and an **Action**.
4. **Round Ends** — Apply any end-of-round effects.
5. **Repeat** — Continue until one side flees or is defeated.

### Surprise

Attackers may gain surprise through:
- A successful **Stealth** Test (potentially opposed by **Perception**).
- Attacking from behind, in darkness, through fog, or from above.
- Exploiting distractions (crowds, loud noises, etc.).
- Attacking completely unprepared defenders (automatic surprise).

Surprised characters gain the [Surprised](/codex/condition/condition_surprised) Condition. A **Resolve** point may be spent to remove it immediately.
