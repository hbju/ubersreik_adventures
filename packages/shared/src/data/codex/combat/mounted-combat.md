## Mounted Combat

- For **Move** purposes, a rider uses their **mount's Movement**.
- Melee attacks from a mounted rider against a **smaller-than-mount** target gain **+20 to hit**.
- When targeting a mounted character, choose to hit **rider** or **mount**. Targeting the rider while smaller than the mount incurs an additional **–10** to the Melee Test.
- A mount without the **Skittish** Trait acts as a full combatant.
- **Charging** mounted uses the mount's **Strength** and **Size** for damage.
- **[Dodge](/codex/skills/dodge)** while mounted suffers **–20** (negated by the **Trick Riding** Talent).
