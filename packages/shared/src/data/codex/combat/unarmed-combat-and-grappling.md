## Unarmed Combat and Grappling

A successful **Melee (Brawling)** Test functions like any other combat Test, with one additional option: **Grappling**.

Declare intent to Grapple **before** rolling. If you win the Opposed Test, both combatants are Grappling and your opponent gains the [Entangled](/codex/condition/condition_entangled) Condition.

**On subsequent turns while Grappling:**
- You may **break the Grapple** (without needing a Test) if your [Advantage](/codex/mechanics/advantage) is higher — at the cost of dropping [Advantage](/codex/mechanics/advantage) to 0.
- Otherwise, make an **[Opposed Test](/codex/mechanics/opposed-tests) — Strength vs. Strength** as your Action:
  - **Win:** Deal **SB + SL Damage** (ignoring AP), or manipulate [Entangled](/codex/condition/condition_entangled) Conditions.
  - **Lose:** Opponent gains **+1 Advantage**; you can only struggle.

Outsiders gain **+20 to hit** the grappler with the lower Advantage, and **+10 to hit** the grappler with the higher Advantage.
