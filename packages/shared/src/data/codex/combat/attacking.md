## Attacking

### Default Combat Difficulty

All combat Tests are assumed to be **Challenging (+0)** unless otherwise stated.

### Step 1: Roll to Hit

**Melee:** Make an **[Opposed Test](/codex/mechanics/opposed-tests)** using your **Melee** Skill against your opponent's **Melee** Skill. The higher **SL** wins. The winner gains **+1 [Advantage](/codex/mechanics/advantage)**; the loser ends their Action.

**Ranged:** Make a **Ranged** Test for your weapon. Success = hit and **+1 Advantage**. Failure = miss (opponent does not gain Advantage in ranged combat).

> **Opposing a Melee Attack:** You may oppose melee attacks with more than just **Melee** — [Dodge](/codex/skills/dodge) is the most common alternative. Other Skills (e.g., **[Intimidate](/codex/skills/intimidate)**, **[Charm](/codex/skills/charm)**) may be used with GM approval, though doing so forgoes the chance to score a Critical Hit.

### Step 2: Determine Hit Location

Reverse the digits of the to-hit roll and consult the **Hit Locations** table.

| Roll | Location |
|---|---|
| 01–09 | Head |
| 10–24 | Left Arm (Secondary Arm) |
| 25–44 | Right Arm (Primary Arm) |
| 45–79 | Body |
| 80–89 | Left Leg |
| 90–00 | Right Leg |

> **Example:** A roll of 23 to hit reverses to 32 — a hit on the Right Arm.

### Step 3: Determine Damage

**Damage = Weapon Damage + SL**

Each weapon has a **Weapon Damage** value (usually **Strength Bonus** for melee; a fixed number for ranged).

### Step 4: Apply Damage

**Wounds Suffered = Damage − (Toughness Bonus + Armour Points)**

Minimum 1 Wound is always lost, even if the calculation would result in 0 or less. If Wounds lost exceed the target's remaining **Wounds**, they take a **Critical Wound** and gain the [Prone](/codex/condition/condition_prone) Condition.
