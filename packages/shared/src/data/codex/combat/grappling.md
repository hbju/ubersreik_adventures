# Grappling

You can attempt to Grapple an opponent instead of striking them with a weapon. To do so, you must have at least **one hand free** and make an Opposed **Melee (Brawling)** Test.

## Initiating a Grapple

1. Declare a Grapple attempt on your turn.
2. Make an **Opposed Melee (Brawling) Test** against the opponent.
3. If you win, the opponent gains the [Entangled](/codex/condition/condition_entangled) Condition.

## While Grappled

The entangled character:
- Cannot **Move**
- Suffers **-10** to all Tests involving movement (including further grappling attempts)
- Can attempt to break free using an **Opposed Strength Test** on their turn

## Grappling Actions

While you have an opponent Grappled (Entangled), you can perform one of these actions on your turn:

| Action | Test | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Maintain** | Opposed Melee (Brawling) | Keep the target Entangled another round |
| **Throw** | Opposed Melee (Brawling) | Target gains [Prone](/codex/condition/condition_prone) and you release |
| **Choke** | Opposed Melee (Brawling) | Deal SB Damage, ignoring AP on the head |
| **Takedown** | Opposed Melee (Brawling) | Both gain Prone; loser also gains [Stunned](/codex/condition/condition_stunned) |

## Relevant Talents

- **In-Fighter** — Provides bonus SL when Grappling
- **Wrestling** — Allows grappling of larger creatures without penalty
- **Dirty Fighting** — Bonus to surprise grapple attacks
