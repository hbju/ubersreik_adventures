# Critical Hits

When you score a Critical Hit in combat, you inflict a lasting wound on your opponent. Critical Hits occur when an attack reduces a target to **0 Wounds** (or below).

## When a Critical Hit Occurs

1. The attack reduces the target to **0 Wounds**.
2. Any **excess damage** becomes the severity of the Critical Hit.
3. Roll on the **Critical Hit Table** for the hit location.
4. Apply the result — effects range from scars and pain to severed limbs and death.

## Critical Wound Severity

| Excess Damage | Severity |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Minor |
| 3–4 | Moderate |
| 5–6 | Serious |
| 7–8 | Major |
| 9+ | Fatal or Catastrophic |

## Hit Locations

Roll **d100** and reverse the digits:

| Roll | Location |
|---|---|
| 01–09 | Head |
| 10–24 | Left Arm |
| 25–44 | Right Arm |
| 45–79 | Body |
| 80–89 | Left Leg |
| 90–00 | Right Leg |

## Treatment

Critical Wounds require medical attention. A **Heal Test** can stabilise a Critical Wound, but many have lasting effects that require extended rest or magical healing.
