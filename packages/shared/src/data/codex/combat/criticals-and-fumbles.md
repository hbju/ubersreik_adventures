## Criticals and Fumbles

### Criticals

Any successful **Melee** or **Ranged** Test that also rolls a **double** is a **Critical**. The opponent immediately receives a **Critical Wound** (see [Injury](/codex/mechanics/injury)). **SL** is still calculated normally.

> **Optional Rule — Deathblow:** Killing a melee opponent in a single blow lets you move into their space and attack another opponent. You may do this a number of times equal to your **Weapon Skill Bonus** per Turn.

### Fumbles

Any **failed** combat Test that also rolls a **double** is a **Fumble**. Roll on the **Oops! Table**.

#### Oops! Table

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 01–20 | Hit yourself — lose 1 Wound (ignores **TB** and **AP**). |
| 21–40 | Weapon jars or malfunctions — weapon suffers 1 Damage; act last next Round. |
| 41–60 | Misjudged manoeuvre — your Action suffers **–10** next Round. |
| 61–70 | Stumble badly — lose your next Move. |
| 71–80 | Drop or mishandle weapon — miss your next Action. |
| 81–90 | Overextend and twist your ankle — suffer a **Torn Muscle (Minor)** injury (counts as a Critical Wound). |
| 91–00 | Completely mess up — hit 1 random ally in range, using the units die as SL. If no ally is reachable, hit yourself in the face and gain a [Stunned](/codex/condition/condition_stunned) Condition. |

> **Misfires:** If using a Blackpowder, Engineering, or Explosive weapon and roll a Fumble on an **even double** (00, 88, etc.), the weapon explodes. Full Damage to your Primary Arm using the units die as SL; weapon is destroyed.

> **Fumbling but Still Winning:** In an Opposed Test, it's possible to Fumble and still win if your SL exceeds your opponent's. You still roll on the Oops! Table.
