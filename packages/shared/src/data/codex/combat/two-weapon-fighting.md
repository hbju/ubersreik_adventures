# Two-Weapon Fighting

Fighting with a weapon in each hand allows more flexibility in combat — but it is not easy!

## Rules

When fighting with two weapons, you may use your **off-hand weapon** to make a secondary attack or defend:

- **Secondary Attack:** After your main attack, you may make a second attack using your off-hand weapon. This attack suffers **-20** unless you have the **Ambidextrous** Talent.
- **Defensive Use:** You may use the off-hand weapon to **parry** incoming melee attacks, granting **+10** to your defensive Melee Test.

## Ambidextrous Talent

Characters with the [Ambidextrous](/codex/talent/ambidextrous) Talent reduce or eliminate the **off-hand penalty**:
- **Rank 1:** Off-hand penalty reduced to **-10**
- **Rank 2+:** Off-hand penalty eliminated

## Dual Wielder Talent

The [Dual Wielder](/codex/talent/dual-wielder) Talent provides additional benefits:
- Add **+1 SL** to your combined attack when using two weapons

## Weapon Restrictions

- Both weapons must be usable **one-handed**
- A shield counts as an off-hand "weapon" for parrying purposes
