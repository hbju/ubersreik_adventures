## Pulling Your Blows

Declare **before rolling to hit** that you are pulling your blow (using the flat of a blade, a controlled technique, etc.). Wounds are still inflicted normally, but **Critical Damage only occurs if the opponent is reduced to 0 Wounds** — not from doubles.

The following Weapon Qualities are **lost** when pulling blows: **Hack**, **Impact**, **Impale**, and **Penetrating**.

You **may not** pull blows with weapons causing [Ablaze](/codex/conditions/ablaze) Conditions, missile weapons, or magic spells.
