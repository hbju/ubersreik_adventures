## Ranged Combat

Additional rules for ranged attacks:

- Ranged attacks **cannot** be opposed with Melee Skills unless the attacker has a large shield or is at **Point Blank** range (where [Dodge](/codex/skills/dodge) also applies).
- You **cannot** make ranged attacks while **Engaged**, unless using a weapon with the **Pistol** quality.
- If firing a Ranged weapon while Engaged with your target, the target may oppose with any **Melee** Skill.
