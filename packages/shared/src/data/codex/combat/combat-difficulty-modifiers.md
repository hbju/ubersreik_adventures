## Combat Difficulty Modifiers

| Difficulty | Modifier | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Very Easy | +60 | Shooting a Monstrous target; shooting into a crowd of 13+ |
| Easy | +40 | Shooting at Point Blank; shooting an Enormous target; outnumbering 3-to-1; shooting into a group of 7–12 |
| Average | +20 | Shooting a Large target; shooting at Short Range; aiming last action; attacking flanks or rear; outnumbering 2-to-1; attacking [Prone](/codex/condition/condition_prone) target |
| Challenging | +0 | Standard attack; shooting a Human-sized target |
| Difficult | –10 | Attacking while [Prone](/codex/condition/condition_prone); difficult terrain; shooting at Long Range; shooting while moving; small target; soft cover |
| Hard | –20 | Called shot to specific location; enclosed space with long weapon; fog/mist/shadow; extreme weather; [Dodge](/codex/skills/dodge) while mounted; darkness (melee); shooting a little target; off-hand weapon; medium cover |
| Very Hard | –30 | Attacking in deep snow/water; tiny target; Extreme Range; shooting in darkness; hard cover |

### Combining Difficulties

- Multiple **penalties** stack up to a maximum of **–30 (Very Hard)**.
- Multiple **bonuses** stack up to a maximum of **+60 (Very Easy)**.
- A penalty and a bonus **cancel each other out** mathematically.

> **Example:** Attacking while in deep snow (–30) against a [Prone](/codex/condition/condition_prone) target (+20) results in a **Difficult (–10)** Test overall.
