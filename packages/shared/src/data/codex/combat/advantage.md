> **Variant Rule — Up in Arms:** This campaign uses the **Group Advantage** rules from *Up in Arms*. These replace the individual Advantage rules found in the core rulebook (WFRP p. 164–165).

## Overview

Rather than each combatant tracking their own Advantage individually, the Group Advantage system uses two shared **Advantage Pools**: one for the **Ally** side and one for the **Adversary** side. Advantage is gained and spent from these pools rather than attached to individual characters.

---

## Gaining Advantage

Advantage is added to the appropriate pool by winning Opposed Tests in combat, activating relevant Talents, and through the **Initial Advantage** rules at the start of a fight (see below).

---

## Spending Advantage

Advantage can be spent from the relevant pool on **any player or creature's turn**. You do not need to consult with your allies before spending, though courtesy is rarely a vice.

Advantage can also be spent to activate **Creature Traits** as per the core rulebook (pp. 338–343).

### Advantage Spend Table

| Cost | Name | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Batter** | Special Action | Perform an **Opposed Strength Test** against one opponent. Winner by highest SL wins. **Win:** opponent gains the [Prone](/codex/conditions/prone) Condition and you gain +1 Advantage. **Lose:** opponent gains +1 Advantage and your Action ends. Winning this test does not itself generate Advantage. |
| 1 | **Trick** | Special Action | Perform an **Opposed Agility Test** against one opponent. **Win:** gain +1 Advantage; the GM may also allow you to inflict your choice of [Ablaze](/codex/conditions/ablaze), [Blinded](/codex/conditions/blinded), or [Entangled](/codex/conditions/entangled) if circumstances permit. **Lose:** opponent gains +1 Advantage and your Action ends. The GM may disallow Conditions if you lack a suitable item or have used the same trick before. Winning this test does not itself generate Advantage. |
| 2 | **Additional Effort** | Free Action | Gain a **+10% bonus** to any one Test before you roll it. You may spend additional Advantage for a further **+10% per Advantage** spent (e.g. 3 Advantage for +20%, 4 Advantage for +30%). This Test never generates Advantage. |
| 2 | **Flee from Harm** | Move | Move away from your opponents **without penalty**. This replaces the standard [Disengaging](/codex/mechanics/movement#disengaging) rules. |
| 4 | **Additional Action** | Free Action | Perform an additional Action this turn. This Action never generates Advantage. May only be used **once per turn**. |

---

## Losing Advantage

At the **end of each Round**, the GM assesses which side is dominant:

- If one side has **more combatants**, they are dominant and the other side is suppressed.
- If numbers are **equal**, decide by which side holds a **tactical advantage** (higher ground, surrounded opponents, etc.).

Once decided: move **1 Advantage** from the suppressed pool to the dominant pool. If the suppressed pool has **no Advantage**, the dominant pool gains **1 Advantage** instead.

---

## Initial Advantage

At the start of combat, the GM may seed the Advantage Pools to reflect tactical positioning before the first action is taken. Only the **highest modifier** in each category applies. Advantage is awarded to the pool of the side that benefits.

| Circumstance | Advantage Awarded |
|---|---|
| **Manoeuvrability:** One side has superior movement (e.g. mounted vs. infantry, or facing giant spiders in trees) | 2 |
| **Outnumbering:** More combatants, but less than 2:1 | 1 |
| **Outnumbering:** 2:1 ratio | 2 |
| **Outnumbering:** 3:1 ratio | 3 |
| **Surprise:** One side achieved an unexpected assault | 2 |
| **Terrain:** Light cover/fortification, or an advantageous position such as a hill | 1 |
| **Terrain:** Heavy cover or a key position such as a bridge | 2 |
| **Threat:** A dangerous threat such as a Warpfire Thrower, Ogre, or Troll | 1 |
| **Threat:** A very dangerous threat, a match for several foes (Organ Gun, Manticore, Griffon) | 3 |
| **Threat:** An extremely dangerous threat, a match for a dozen lesser foes (Dragon, Greater Daemon) | 5 |

> **Example:** Five adventurers have snuck up on ten Goblins and their Shaman riding a Manticore. The **Ally Pool** gains 2 Advantage for surprise. The **Adversary Pool** gains 2 for outnumbering the adventurers 2:1, and 3 for the Manticore — a total of 5 Advantage.
