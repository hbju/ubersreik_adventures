## Special Combat Cases

### Helpless Targets

Melee Tests against **sleeping, unconscious, or otherwise helpless** targets **automatically succeed**.

### Shooting into a Group

| Group Size | Modifier |
|---|---|
| 3–6 targets | Average (+20) |
| 7–12 targets | Easy (+40) |
| 13+ targets | Very Easy (+60) |

Hits are randomised among all likely targets.

### Outnumbering

| Ratio | Melee Hit Bonus |
|---|---|
| 2-to-1 | +20 |
| 3-to-1 | +40 |

All outnumbered opponents also lose **1 Advantage** at the end of every Round.

> **Optional Rule — Shooting into Melee:** Targeting a specific engaged combatant imposes **–20** to hit. If the roll would have succeeded without the penalty, a random ally of the target is hit instead. Shooting without caring about friendly fire gives **+20 to +60**, as per Shooting into a Group.

### Size Modifiers

|---|---|---|---|
