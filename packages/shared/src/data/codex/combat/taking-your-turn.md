## Taking Your Turn

Each turn, a combatant has one **Move** and one **Action**, taken in any order (or simultaneously). Skipping either is permitted, but there are no carry-overs to the next Round.

### Move

Use your **Movement Characteristic** as a guide to distance covered. Reaching difficult terrain (climbing, jumping) requires an **Action** and a relevant Test (e.g., **Climb** or **Athletics**). **Charging** into melee when not already **Engaged** grants **+1 [Advantage](/codex/mechanics/advantage)**.

### Action

An Action can be anything within a character's capability and the physical constraints of the scene. If it requires a Test, the GM says so and narrates the result.

#### Combat Actions

The following Actions replace or supplement those in the core rulebook.

**Assess:** Use a Skill creatively to gain an advantage in the fight. Narrate how it applies and make a **Dramatic Test**. The GM may apply a bonus or penalty if the Skill is particularly suited or unsuited to the situation. On a **success**: gain **2 Advantage**. On a **success with 6+ SL**: gain **3 Advantage**.

**Attack:** A standard melee or ranged attack, resolved as per the core rulebook (WFRP p. 158).

**Defend:** Choose a Skill or Characteristic to use defensively (such as [Dodge](/codex/skills/dodge) or Agility). Gain **+20 to defensive Tests** using that Skill until the start of your next turn.

**Special:** Actions from Skills or Talents — grappling, intimidating, casting a spell, using the Dual Wielder Talent, staunching a wound with bandages, etc.

**Sprint:** Use your Action to sprint. Make an **Average (+20) [Athletics](/codex/skills/athletics)** Test. On success, move your normal Move distance **plus** your Run movement **plus SL** in additional yards.

> **Example:** A character with Move 4 sprints and rolls –2 SL — they move an additional 14 yards (Run 16 – 2 SL = 14).

**Charging** grants a **+10 bonus** to the first Melee Test you initiate after completing your move.


### Free Actions

Minor activities — shouting a warning, drawing a weapon, drinking a potion — may be declared as **Free Actions** at the GM's discretion. As a general rule: if it requires a Test, it is an **Action**, not a Free Action.

> **Optional Rule — On the Defensive:** As your Action, nominate a Skill to use defensively. You gain **+20** to defensive Tests using that Skill until your next Turn.
