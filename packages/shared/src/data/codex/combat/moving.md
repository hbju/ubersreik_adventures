## Moving

### Charging

If not already Engaged and target is between your **Walk** and **Run** distance away, you may **Charge** — your Action must be a Melee attack. You gain **+1 [Advantage](/codex/mechanics/advantage)**.

### Disengaging

**Flee from Harm** | Move away from your opponents **without penalty**, using 2 Advantage (see [Advantage](/codex/combat/advantage) for details). 

**Use [Dodge](/codex/skills/dodge):** Make an **[Opposed](/codex/mechanics/opposed-tests) [Dodge](/codex/skills/dodge)/Melee Test**. Success: gain **+1 Advantage** and move freely. Failure: each winning opponent gains **+1 Advantage** and you remain trapped.

### Fleeing

Turning to flee allows your opponent **1 free, unopposed Melee attack** (with **+20 to hit**), and they gain **+1 Advantage** immediately. After the attack resolves, you may move up to your **Run** distance away. If wounded by the free attack, pass a **Challenging (+0) [Cool](/codex/skills/cool)** Test or gain a [Broken](/codex/condition/condition_broken) Condition (plus 1 additional [Broken](/codex/condition/condition_broken) per SL below 0).

### Running

Use your **Action** for an **Average (+20) [Athletics](/codex/skills/athletics)** Test. On success, you sprint your **Run movement + SL** in additional yards.

### Climbing

Climbing a ladder or easy surface requires **no Test** but costs movement at **half rate** (2 yards of Movement per 1 yard climbed). To climb faster, use your Action for an **Average (+20) Climb Test** to ascend an additional **Movement + SL** yards. Difficult surfaces may require the **Scale Sheer Surface** Talent.

### Leaping and Falling

**Leaping:** You can leap your **Movement in feet** without a Test. Further jumps require an **Average (+20) [Athletics](/codex/skills/athletics)** Test with a run-up (Challenging +0 without one). Each SL adds **1 foot** to the leap; +0 SL adds **6 inches**.

**Falling:** Suffer **1d10 Damage + 3 Damage per yard fallen**, reduced by **Toughness Bonus** (not AP). An **Average (+20) [Athletics](/codex/skills/athletics)** Test when jumping deliberately reduces the effective fall distance by **1 yard + 1 per SL**. If Wounds lost exceed your **Toughness Bonus**, also gain the [Prone](/codex/condition/condition_prone) Condition.
