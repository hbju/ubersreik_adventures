## What's Inside

The Codex gathers every rule, talent, skill, career, condition and item quality in one searchable place. Browse by category in the sidebar or press **Ctrl + K** to fuzzy-search anything.

## Quick Start

- **Search** — Press `Ctrl+K` (or `Cmd+K` on Mac) to open the Command Palette and type what you're looking for.
- **Browse** — Use the sidebar on the left to explore entries grouped by category.
- **Bookmark** — Click the ☆ star on any entry to save it for quick access. Bookmarks appear at the top of the sidebar.
- **Contextual lookup** — Dotted-underlined talent and condition names on character sheets are clickable. Click one to see its full description in a popup.
- **Cross-links** — Many entries link to related rules. Click a link to jump straight to that entry.

## Key Mechanics Refresher

### Making a Test
Roll **d100** against a target number (Characteristic + Skill advances + modifiers). If the roll is ≤ the target, you succeed. **Success Levels (SL)** = tens digit of target − tens digit of roll.

### Advantage
You gain **+1 Advantage** each time you win an Opposed Test in combat or achieve a surprising success. Each point of Advantage adds **+10** to your next relevant Test. Advantage resets to 0 when you lose an Opposed Test or the combat situation shifts against you.

> *See [Advantage](/codex/md:mechanics/advantage) for the full rules.*

### Opposed Tests
Both sides roll a Test. Compare SLs — the higher SL wins. On a tie the defender wins, unless both fail, in which case the side with the better SL wins.

> *See [Opposed Tests](/codex/md:mechanics/opposed-tests) for details.*

## Tips for GMs

- Use the Codex to quickly look up a talent's effect in the middle of a session instead of flipping through the rulebook.
- Bookmark talents and conditions that come up frequently for your party.
- The **Career** entries list every skill and talent available at each level — handy when players ask about advancement options.

## Tips for Players

- Before your session, browse your career entry to plan your next advances.
- Bookmark conditions your character is prone to (e.g. *Fatigued* for heavy-armour fighters, *Broken* for low-Willpower characters).
- Use `Ctrl+K` to search for a talent name when you're unsure what it does during play.
