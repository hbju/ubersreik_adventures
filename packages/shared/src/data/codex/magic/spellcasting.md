# Spellcasting Rules

*These rules supersede those in the WFRP Core Rulebook.*

---

## Spell Types

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Petty Spells** | Simple tricks involving small amounts of magic. |
| **Lore Spells** | Exclusive to a particular magical tradition. Include Arcane Spells. |
| **Chaos Spells** | Practised by those who have sold their souls to Chaos. |

---

## Second Sight

The **Second Sight** Talent is required to perceive the Winds of Magic. It functions like a sense — it **cannot be switched off**. Characters may be overwhelmed by the Winds even when they do not wish to be.

Second Sight can be used to assist Intuition, Perception, Track, and similar Tests when sensing the Winds.

---

## Memorising Spells

Spellcasters memorise spells by spending the XP listed in the **Petty Magic** or **Arcane Magic** Talents.

---

## Grimoires

Grimoires typically contain **4 spells** from the same Lore. A spellcaster may cast a spell from a grimoire if the spell belongs to a Lore they possess, but:

- Both hands must be free to flick through the pages.
- The spell's **CN is doubled**.

---

## The Casting Test

To cast a spell, make a **Language (Magick) Test** (the Casting Test).

If successful, compare the SL to the spell's **CN (Casting Number)**. If SL ≥ CN, the spell is cast as described. If SL < CN, the spell fails.

### Critical Casting
Rolling **doubles on a success** causes a Critical Casting. Unless you have **Instinctive Diction**, roll on the **[Minor Miscast Table](/codex/rules/miscast-tables)**. You may also choose **one** of the following:

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Critical Cast** | If the spell deals Damage, it also inflicts a Critical Wound. |
| **Total Power** | The spell is cast even if you didn't achieve enough SL for the CN. Can be dispelled normally. |
| **Unstoppable Force** | The spell is cast (if SL ≥ CN) and **cannot be dispelled**. |

### Fumbled Casting
Rolling **doubles on a failure** triggers a Fumble. Roll on the **[Minor Miscast Table](/codex/rules/miscast-tables)**.

---

## Spellcasting Limitations

- A spellcaster must **speak or sing** to cast. If their voice is inhibited, increase Casting Test difficulty by one step.
- A spell must **end or be dispelled** before the same spell can be cast again.
- When spells provide bonuses/penalties, only the **best bonus** and **worst penalty** apply — they do not stack.
- The caster must have **Line of Sight** to their target unless stated otherwise.

---

## Advantage and Magic

Advantage applies to **Casting Tests** but **not Channelling Tests**.

If a target already had a spell from the **same Lore** cast on it that Round, the caster gains **+1 Advantage** when casting spells targeting that creature.

---

## Ingredients

Using an ingredient during spellcasting reduces the risk of Miscasts. Choose one:

- A **Major Miscast** is reduced to a Minor one.
- A **Minor Miscast** is reduced to no effect.

Ingredients must be bought for a **specific spell** and cost the **CN in silver shillings**.

---

## Magic Missiles

When a magic missile is successfully cast, **reverse the dice** rolled on the Casting Test and consult the Hit Locations Table to find where the target is struck.

**Damage** = Caster's WP Bonus + spell's listed Damage, reduced by Toughness Bonus and Armour Points as normal.

---

## Touch Spells in Combat

To touch an unwilling target, after a successful Casting Test, make an **Opposed Melee (Brawling) Test** (opposed by Melee or Dodge). For magic missiles, the Brawling Test is reversed to determine Hit Location.

Enchanted Staffs and similar items replace the Brawling Test with the most appropriate Melee Test (e.g. Melee (Polearm)). This test determines contact only — it does not deal additional Damage.

---

## Random Vortices

Random vortex spells summon a destructive maelstrom that moves out of the caster's control once unleashed.

1. On a successful Casting Test, the vortex appears **adjacent to but not touching** the caster.
2. The caster chooses a direction and makes an **Average (+20) Willpower Test**:
   - **Success:** The vortex moves 2d10 yards in the chosen direction.
   - **Failure:** The vortex moves in a random direction.
3. Each subsequent round, at the end of the caster's turn, roll on the **Vortex Movement Table** and move the vortex 2d10 yards. Everything in its path is affected. Rolling a **1** causes the vortex to disappear prematurely.

### Vortex Movement Table

| Roll | Map-Based | Abstract |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vortex disappears | Vortex disappears |
| 2 | North | Caster chooses direction |
| 3 | Northeast | Caster chooses direction |
| 4 | East | Caster chooses direction |
| 5 | Vortex remains | Caster chooses direction |
| 6 | Southeast | Vortex remains in place |
| 7 | South | GM chooses direction |
| 8 | Southwest | GM chooses direction |
| 9 | West | GM chooses direction |
| 10 | Northwest | GM chooses direction |

## Repelling the Winds

Metal and leather armour interfere with spellcasting. Spellcasters suffer **–1 SL** to all Casting and Channelling Tests for every Armour Point on the **location with the most armour**.

**Exceptions:**
- Casters with **Arcane Magic (Metal)** may wear metal armour without penalty.
- Casters with **Arcane Magic (Beasts)** may wear leather armour without penalty.
- Chaos Sorcerers may wear Chaos Armour without penalty.

---

## Malignant Influences

If a Character makes a Casting or Channelling Test while **near a Corrupting Influence**, any **failed roll** requires a roll on the Minor Miscast Table. If the roll would already trigger the Minor Miscast Table (e.g. Fumbled Channelling), roll on the **Major Miscast Table** instead.

Spellcasters using **Dark Lores** do not count as Malignant Influences to themselves.

---

## Warpstone

Using Warpstone **doubles all SL** generated on Casting and Channelling Tests, but counts as a **Malignant Influence**. Track consumption: **1 gram of warpstone** provides roughly **20 CN** of magical energy before being spent.

---

## Multiple Arcane Lores

An **Elf spellcaster** may learn a number of Arcane Lores equal to their **Willpower Bonus**. They may not purchase a new Arcane Magic Talent until they have spent at least **20 Advances** in the Channelling Skill of, and learned at least **8 spells** from, the previous Lore.

Any spellcaster may learn **one Dark Lore** in addition to another Lore.
