# Casting Spells

Magic in the Old World is channelled from the Winds of Magic (the Aethyr). Only those born with the Talent to perceive and manipulate these winds can cast spells.

## The Casting Process

1. **Declare** the spell you are casting and the target.
2. Choose the number of **Success Levels (SL)** required. Most spells have a **Casting Number (CN)** that must be met or exceeded.
3. Make a **Language (Magick)** Test against your Willpower.
4. If your SL meets or exceeds the CN, the spell is successfully cast.

## Casting Roll

Make a **Willpower Test** using **Language (Magick)**:

- **SL ≥ CN:** Spell is cast successfully.
- **SL < CN:** Spell fizzles — no effect, but no backlash either.

## Overcasting

If your SL exceeds the CN, you may apply the extra SL to enhance the spell:

| Extra SL | Enhancement |
|---|---|
| +2 SL | Increase Range |
| +2 SL | Increase Duration |
| +2 SL | Increase targets by 1 |
| +2 SL | Increase Damage by +3 |

## Miscasts

If you roll a **double** on a failed casting attempt, a **Minor Miscast** occurs. If you roll a double on a casting attempt where you also pushed (channelled), a **Major Miscast** occurs.

See [Miscasts](/codex/magic/miscasts) for the miscast tables.
