# Channelling

Channelling allows a spellcaster to accumulate magical energy over multiple turns before releasing a spell, making it easier to meet a spell's Casting Number (CN).

## How Channelling Works

1. On your turn, declare you are **Channelling** instead of casting.
2. Make a **Channelling Test** (using the appropriate Wind Lore skill, e.g., Channelling (Aqshy) for fire magic).
3. If successful, gain **SL** as stored energy. This is added to your next Casting attempt.
4. You may continue Channelling on subsequent turns to accumulate more SL.

## Channelling Risks

- **Each Round** spent Channelling carries risk: if you roll a **double**, you suffer a **Minor Miscast** immediately.
- If you are attacked while Channelling:
  - You must pass a **Cool Test** or lose all accumulated SL.
  - If you lose concentration, the gathered energy dissipates harmlessly.

## Using Channelled Energy

When you finally cast:
- Add all accumulated **Channelling SL** to your **Casting SL**.
- This combined total is compared to the spell's **CN**.

## Limits

There is no hard limit to how many rounds you can spend Channelling, but each round increases the risk of a Miscast and leaves you vulnerable.
