# Miscasts

When magic goes awry, the caster suffers a Miscast. Miscasts range from minor inconveniences to catastrophic reality-warping events.

## When a Miscast Occurs

- **Minor Miscast:** You roll a **double** on a *failed* casting or channelling attempt (e.g., 11, 22, 33…).
- **Major Miscast:** You roll a **double** on a *failed* casting attempt after having **Channelled** power, OR if you roll a double that is also a critical failure.

## Minor Miscast Table

Roll **d10**:

| Roll | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Witchfire — eerie lights surround you for 1d10 rounds. +1 SL on Intimidation, −1 SL on Fellowship tests. |
| 2 | Magical Surge — the spell works, but at the caster's minimum CN. Apply the spell effect. |
| 3 | Aethyric Feedback — gain 1 [Stunned](/codex/condition/condition_stunned) Condition. |
| 4 | Overcharge — gain 1 [Fatigued](/codex/condition/condition_fatigued) Condition. |
| 5 | Knocked Out — gain 1 [Prone](/codex/condition/condition_prone) Condition. |
| 6 | Multiplied SL — the spell goes off with double SL (for better or worse). |
| 7 | Foul Stench — a terrible odour emanates from you for 1d10 minutes. |
| 8 | Aethyric Echo — the same spell goes off again next round, targeting a random nearby creature. |
| 9 | Power Drain — lose 1d10 Channelling SL (if any). |
| 10 | Roll on the **Major Miscast** table instead. |

## Major Miscast Table

Major Miscasts are far more destructive. They may cause mutations, summoning of daemons, or even tears in reality. Consult the full Major Miscast table in the rulebook (p. 235).

## Corruption

Some Miscasts cause the caster to gain **Corruption Points**. If corruption exceeds your threshold (WP Bonus + TB), a **Mutation** Test is required.
