# Miscast Tables

Roll when required by the [Spellcasting](/codex/rules/spellcasting) or [Channelling](/codex/rules/channelling-overcasting) rules.

---

## Minor Miscast Table

| Roll | Effect |
|---|---|
| 01–05 | **Witchsign.** The next living creature born within 1 mile is mutated. |
| 06–10 | **Soured Milk.** All milk within 1d100 yards goes sour instantly. |
| 11–15 | **Blight.** WP Bonus fields within WP Bonus miles suffer a blight; all crops rot overnight. |
| 16–20 | **Soulwax.** Gain 1 Deafened Condition, removable only with a successful Average (+20) Heal Test. |
| 21–25 | **Freezing Breath.** Temperature drops suddenly. Creatures within WP Bonus yards make a Challenging (+0) Endurance Test or suffer –10 BS, Ag, and Dex for 1 minute. |
| 26–30 | **Unfasten.** Every buckle and lace on your person undoes itself — belts fall, pouches open, armour slips. |
| 31–35 | **Wayward Garb.** Your clothes writhe. Gain 1 Entangled Condition (Strength 1d10×5 to resist). |
| 36–40 | **Curse of Temperance.** All alcohol within 1d100 yards goes bitter and undrinkable. |
| 41–45 | **Cloyed Tongue.** Suffer –10 to all Language Tests (including Casting Tests) for 1d10 Rounds. |
| 46–50 | **Driven to Distraction.** If in combat, gain the Surprised Condition. Otherwise, you are completely startled and unable to concentrate for a few moments. |
| 51–55 | **Unholy Visions.** Gain a Blinded Condition. Fail a Challenging (+0) Cool Test and gain another. |
| 56–60 | **Hexeyes.** Eyes turn an unnatural colour for 1d10 hours. Gain 1 Blinded Condition (cannot be resolved). Lose Magical Sense and Second Sight during this time. |
| 61–65 | **Rupture.** Nose, eyes, and ears bleed. Gain a Bleeding Condition. |
| 66–70 | **Fell Whispers.** GM may choose two reversed symbols from the Symbols Table. Pass Average (+20) WP Test or gain 1 Corruption. |
| 71–75 | **The Horror!** GM selects a reversed symbol. Pass Hard (–20) Cool Test or gain 1 Broken Condition. |
| 76–80 | **Curse of Corruption.** Gain 1 Corruption point. |
| 81–85 | **Intestinal Rebellion.** You soil yourself. Gain 1 Fatigued Condition, removable only after cleaning up. |
| 86–90 | **Marked by Magic.** Gain an arcane mark associated with your Wind. See the relevant Marks table for your Lore. If none exists, roll on the Major Miscast Table instead. |
| 91–95 | **Multiplying Misfortune.** Roll twice on this table, rerolling results between 91–00. |
| 96–00 | **Cascading Chaos.** Roll on the Major Miscast Table. |

## Major Miscast Table

| Roll | Effect |
|---|---|
| 01–05 | **Ghostly Voices.** Everyone within WP yards hears seductive Chaos whispering. All sentient creatures pass Average (+20) Cool or gain 1 Corruption. |
| 06–10 | **Aethyric Shock.** Suffer 1d10 Wounds (ignoring TB and AP). Fail Average (+20) Endurance and also gain Stunned. |
| 11–15 | **Death Walker.** For 1d10 hours, all plant life near you withers and dies. |
| 16–20 | **Double Trouble.** The spell's effect occurs elsewhere within 1d10 miles, with consequences at GM's discretion. |
| 21–25 | **Soulfire.** Gain an Ablaze Condition, wreathed in unholy flames in your Lore's colour. |
| 26–30 | **Speak in Tongues.** Gabble unintelligibly for 1d10 rounds. Cannot speak or make Casting Tests. |
| 31–35 | **Swarmed.** Engaged by a swarm of aethyric creatures (GM's choice). After 1d10 rounds, if not destroyed, the swarm retreats. |
| 36–40 | **Ragdoll.** Flung 1d10 yards in a random direction. Take 1d10 Wounds (ignoring AP) and gain the Prone Condition. |
| 41–45 | **Limb Frozen.** One random limb is frozen for 1d10 hours, useless as if Amputated. |
| 46–50 | **Darkling Sight.** Lose Second Sight for 1d10 hours. Channelling Tests suffer –20 for the duration. |
| 51–55 | **Chaotic Foresight.** Gain a bonus pool of 1d10 Fortune points (may exceed maximum). Each time you spend one, gain 1 Corruption. Leftover points are lost at session's end. GM assigns reversed symbols equal to WP Bonus. |
| 56–60 | **Levitation.** Float 1d10 yards above ground for 1d10 minutes. Refer to falling rules (WFRP p.166) when it ends. |
| 61–65 | **Regurgitation.** Gain the Stunned Condition for 1d10 Rounds. |
| 66–70 | **Chaos Quake.** All creatures within 1d100 yards pass Average (+20) Athletics or gain the Prone Condition. |
| 71–75 | **Forgetfulness.** The spell being cast is lost from memory. If casting from a grimoire, that page bursts into flames. |
| 76–80 | **Traitor's Heart.** The Dark Gods tempt you. If you attack or betray an ally to the fullest extent, regain all Fortune points. If another Character loses a Fate point as a result, gain +1 Fate. |
| 81–85 | **Foul Enfeeblement.** Gain 1 Corruption point, a Prone Condition, and a Fatigued Condition. |
| 86–90 | **Hellish Stench.** Gain the Distracting Creature Trait for 1d10 hours. |
| 91–95 | **Power Drain.** Cannot use the Talent used to cast the spell (usually Arcane Magic) for 1d10 minutes. |
| 96–00 | **Aethyric Feedback.** Everyone within WP Bonus yards (friend and foe) suffers 1d10 Wounds (ignoring TB and AP) and gains the Prone Condition. If there are no targets in range, your head explodes instantly. |
