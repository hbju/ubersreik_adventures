# Channelling & Overcasting

---

## Channelling

Characters can gather magical power to reduce the CN of a spell. To channel, declare which Wind you are drawing from and make an **Extended Channelling Test** (using the Channelling Skill) each turn.

- Each SL generated **reduces the CN of one spell by 1** (minimum 0).
- Once ready, make a **Casting Test** on your turn at the reduced CN.
- Any leftover channelled power is **lost once you cast**.
- If the Casting Test **fails**, all stored power is lost and you roll on the **[Minor Miscast Table](/codex/rules/miscast-tables)**.
- Channelled power **cannot** be used to Overcast — though reducing CN makes Overcasting more likely.
- To safely stop channelling, spend an action to **vent** your gathered power.

### Critical Channelling
Rolling **doubles while channelling** immediately adds bonus SL equal to your **Willpower Bonus** to the Extended Test. However, unless you have the **Aethyric Attunement** Talent, you must also roll on the **[Minor Miscast Table](/codex/rules/miscast-tables)**.

### Fumbled Channelling
Rolling **doubles on a failed** Channelling Test triggers a Fumble. Roll on the **[Minor Miscast Table](/codex/rules/miscast-tables)**.

### Interruptions
If you are distracted while channelling (e.g. take damage or gain the Surprised Condition), make a **Hard (–20) Cool Test**:
- **Failure:** All channelled SL are lost and you roll on the **[Minor Miscast Table](/codex/rules/miscast-tables)**.

---

## Overcasting

If your Casting Test generates **more SL than the spell's CN**, you may spend the excess SL on Overcasting — imbuing the spell with greater power.

**Restrictions:**
- Can improve: Range, Area of Effect, Duration, Targets, or Damage (magic missiles only).
- **Range 'You'** spells cannot target additional creatures. **Range 'Touch'** and **Instant** spells cannot be extended. Spells with no AoE cannot have their area increased.
- Each column may only be accessed **once per casting**. Unused SL are lost.

### Overcast Table

| SL Spent | +Targets | +Damage | +Range | +AoE | +Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | +1 | +1 | ×2 | Listed AoE | Listed Duration |
| 2 | +1 | +2 | ×2 | Listed AoE | ×2 Duration |
| 3 | +1 | +3 | ×2 | ×2 AoE | ×2 Duration |
| 5 | +2 | +4 | ×3 | ×2 AoE | ×2 Duration |
| 8 | +2 | +5 | ×3 | ×2 AoE | ×3 Duration |
| 13 | +2 | +6 | ×3 | ×2 AoE | ×3 Duration |
| 21+ | +3 | +7 | ×4 | ×3 AoE | ×3 Duration |

> **Example:** *Hengus casts Dart (CN 0) with 5 SL. He spends 3 SL on +3 Damage and 1 SL on ×2 Range. The last SL is lost.*

## Dispelling

A spellcaster may attempt to dispel any spell targeting them, or at a point they can see **within Willpower yards**. Only **one spell** may be dispelled per round.

Make an **Opposed Language (Magick) Test** against the caster:
- **Dispeller wins:** The spell is dispelled and has no effect.
- **Caster wins:** Use the opposed SL as the Casting Roll result.

### Dispelling Persistent Spells

To dispel an ongoing spell, spend an action and make an **Extended Language (Magick) Test**. When your accumulated SL reaches the spell's **CN**, it is dispelled.

Multiple spellcasters dispelling the same spell make Tests separately — if using the **same Lore**, they may instead make an **Assisted Test**.

A spellcaster dispelling **their own spell** gains **+1 SL** on the Language (Magick) Test.
