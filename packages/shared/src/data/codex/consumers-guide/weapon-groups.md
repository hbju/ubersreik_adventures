## Melee Weapon Groups

Each Weapon Group requires a separate Skill to master. Without Advances in the relevant Group, Test **[Weapon Skill](/codex/characteristics/weapon-skill)** to hit — you still suffer all Flaws but gain none of the Qualities.

**Cavalry:** Weapons are assumed to be used while mounted. When unmounted, all two-handed Cavalry weapons also count as Two-Handed weapons. Single-handed Cavalry weapons are not normally used on foot.

**Flail:** Unskilled users add the **[Dangerous](/codex/qualities/dangerous)** Flaw, and Qualities do not apply.

**Parry:** Any one-handed weapon with the **[Defensive](/codex/qualities/defensive)** Quality may be used with **[Melee (Parry)](/codex/skills/melee)**. When doing so, you may oppose incoming attacks without the normal **[–20 off-hand penalty](/codex/combat/two-weapon-fighting)**.

## Ranged Weapon Groups

You cannot attempt a Ranged Test for a weapon without the correct Skill specialisation — except as follows:

**Blackpowder and Explosives:** Characters with **[Ranged (Engineering)](/codex/skills/ranged)** can use Blackpowder and Explosive weapons without penalty.

**Crossbows and Throwing:** You may attempt a **[Ranged (Crossbow)](/codex/skills/ranged)** or **[Ranged (Throwing)](/codex/skills/ranged)** Test using your **[Ballistic Skill](/codex/skills/ballistic)**, but the weapon loses all Qualities while retaining its Flaws.

**Engineering:** Characters with **[Ranged (Blackpowder)](/codex/skills/ranged)** may use Engineering weapons, but those weapons lose all Qualities while retaining their Flaws.
