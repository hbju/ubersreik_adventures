## The Cost of Living

Characters maintaining their **Status** must dress, eat, and equip themselves accordingly. As a rough guide, spending around **half your Status value per day** is sufficient to maintain appearances.

| Social Tier | Currency | Lifestyle |
|---|---|---|
| **Brass** | Brass pennies | Simple foods, shared rooms; peers notice if you spend *too much* |
| **Silver** | Silver shillings | Comfortable beds, good food, quality clothing |
| **Gold** | Gold crowns | Luxury foods, satin sheets, extravagant garb |

> **Optional Rule — Tracking Money:** If you prefer not to count every penny, any item costing less than your current Status level is assumed freely purchasable in normal quantities. Beyond that, you may buy one more expensive item per day with a **Haggle** Test, Difficulty set by the GM.
