## Hirelings

Any Career may be hired with GM permission. Pay an amount of coin equal to their **Social Status** for a quick job, or **triple** for a full day's work. Unexpected danger warrants **double pay** unless a **Leadership** Test is passed.

**Hireling profiles** are created by adding **5 Advances** to every Career Characteristic and Skill per Career level, plus **1 Talent per level**.

### Henchmen

Henchmen are permanent NPC companions — Hirelings, friends, pets, or allies who travel with the party indefinitely. Unlike regular Hirelings, Henchmen require **full character sheets** (just like a PC) and are typically attached to one player character.

Henchmen earn **half the XP** of their associated player character (rounding down), which the player may spend on the Henchman's behalf. Beyond being memorable allies, Henchmen can serve as ready-made **replacement PCs** should a character die, rather than creating someone entirely new.
