## Craftsmanship

Items vary in quality. **Item Qualities** make a trapping better but harder to find; **Item Flaws** make it worse but easier to find.

A trapping is a **Quality Trapping** if it has more Qualities than Flaws. For each Quality, **double its price** and reduce Availability **one step**.

A trapping is a **Flawed Trapping** if it has more Flaws than Qualities. For each Flaw, **halve its price** and improve Availability **one step**.

> **Note:** Exotic items are never made more available by having Flaws — they remain Exotic regardless.

> **Optional Rule — Crafting Guilds:** In towns with relevant crafting guilds, each Flaw *reduces* Availability instead of increasing it, and the first Quality does *not* reduce Availability. Prices are modified as normal.

### Item Qualities

**[Durable](/codex/qualities/durable)**
Crafted from strong materials. The item can take **+Durable** Damage points before suffering penalties, and gains a saving throw of **9+** on 1d10 against instant breakage (e.g. from the Trap Blade Quality). Each additional time this Quality is taken, the saving throw improves by 1 (9+ → 8+ → etc.).

> **Example:** Molli's sword has Durable 3 — it absorbs 3 Damage before penalties apply, and saves against breaking on a 6+.

**[Fine](/codex/qualities/fine)**
Meticulously crafted for appearance. A sign of social status. May be taken multiple times; higher ratings are progressively more impressive.

**[Lightweight](/codex/qualities/lightweight)**
Cleverly designed for ease of carrying. Reduce **[Encumbrance](/codex/mechanics/encumbrance)** by 1.

**[Practical](/codex/qualities/practical)**
Expertly crafted with utility in mind. A failed Test using this item receives **+1 SL**. If armour, all penalties for wearing it are reduced by **one Difficulty level** (e.g. –30 → –20).

### Item Flaws

**[Ugly](/codex/flaws/ugly)**
Crafted without aesthetic awareness. May impose **–10** to related **[Fellowship](/codex/skills/fellowship)** Tests.

**[Shoddy](/codex/flaws/shoddy)**
Hastily made. The item breaks on any failed Test rolling a **double**. Shoddy armour breaks if a **Critical Hit** is sustained to a location it protects.

**[Unreliable](/codex/flaws/unreliable)**
Crafted without attention to function. A failed Test using this item receives **–1 SL**. Penalties for wearing Unreliable armour are **doubled**.

**[Bulky](/codex/flaws/bulky)**
Clumsily designed. Increase **[Encumbrance](/codex/mechanics/encumbrance)** by +1 (cannot apply to small trinkets). Bulky clothing and armour have Enc 1 even when worn; armour Fatigue penalties are doubled.
