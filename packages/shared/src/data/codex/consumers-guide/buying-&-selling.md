## Going to Market

Once play begins, the availability of rarer goods is restricted. Finding a harpsichord in a five-house hamlet is not so easy as finding one in Altdorf.

> **Optional Rule:** The GM may prefer to simply roleplay every market visit and dictate what is or isn't available, bypassing these rules entirely.

### Bargaining and Trading

**[Evaluate](/codex/skills/evaluate)** is used to assess item quality (see Craftsmanship), appraise coins for counterfeiting or exchange value, and estimate prices of Rare or Exotic items to within ±10%.

**[Haggle](/codex/skills/haggle)** is used in Opposed Tests between buyer and seller. Most prices are marked up slightly to account for expected haggling. Winning a Haggle Test reduces the price by **10%** (up to **20%** on an Astounding Success or with the **[Dealmaker](/codex/talents/dealmaker)** Talent). Failing badly may cause the vendor to distrust your coinage.

### Selling

Selling works exactly like buying, but in reverse — check for a buyer's Availability as you would check for stock, then Evaluate and Haggle to set a price. The **base selling price** is typically **half the item's listed value**, yielding a quarter to half after negotiation. Merchants or Fences who invest time in **[Gossip](/codex/skills/gossip)** Tests and local market research may find buyers willing to pay up to **80%** of an item's value.

**Lowering the Price:** Each time you halve your asking price, Availability of a buyer increases by **one step** (e.g. Exotic → Rare → Scarce).

> **Example:** Corporal Mauser has a stolen Hochland Longrifle (worth 100 GC) he needs to sell. No immediate buyers exist (Exotic). The base price is 50 GC. He drops the price twice to ~12 GC 10/–, raising Availability two steps from Exotic to Scarce.

### Bartering

Rural and mercantile trade often involves exchanging goods directly. Compare the Availability of items being traded to those being acquired.

#### Bartering Ratios

| Traded → Acquired | Common | Scarce | Rare | Exotic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Common** | 1:1 | 2:1 | 4:1 | 8:1 |
| **Scarce** | 1:2 | 1:1 | 2:1 | 4:1 |
| **Rare** | 1:4 | 1:2 | 1:1 | 2:1 |
| **Exotic** | 1:8 | 1:4 | 1:2 | 1:1 |
