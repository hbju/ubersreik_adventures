## Trade Tools and Workshops

| Item | Cost | Enc | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade Tools | 3 GC | 1 | Rare |
| Workshop | 80 GC | N/A | Exotic |

Trade Tools are required for any **[Trade Test](/codex/skills/trade)** to make or repair items. Workshops are required for larger projects such as creating new trappings.

Sample tool packages by Trade:
- **Apothecary:** Pestle, mortar, spoons, jars, scales; workshop adds oil burners and ingredient shelves.
- **Carpenter:** Hammer, nails, saw, measuring rod, chalk, plumb lines; workshop adds lathe, planes, clamps, lumber.
- **Engraver:** Gravers, punches, wax, acid, hammer, chisels; workshop adds dies and presses.
- **Smith:** Hammer, tongs, punches, swages, bits, augers; workshop adds anvil, furnace, swage block, sledgehammers, metal ingots.
- **Engineer:** Hourglass, measuring rod, fuse cord, drafting compass, T-square; workshop adds pulleys, ropes, drafting tables.
- **Herbalist:** Pestle, mortar, small knives, pruning shears, gloves; workshop adds drying racks, strainers, funnels, bowls, jars.
- **Physician:** Needles, sutures, bandages, scalpel, vinegar, forceps, speculum; workshop adds anatomical drawings, assorted implements, surgery table.

Other Artisan specialisations include: Armourer, Bowyer, Brewer, Candlemaker, Calligrapher, Cartographer, Cobbler, Cook, Cooper, Embalmer, Gem Cutter, Glassblower, Goldsmith, Gunsmith, Jeweller, Leatherworker, Mason, Painter, Potter, Shipwright, Stoneworker, Tailor, Tanner, Vintner, Weaver, Weaponsmith, and Woodcarver.
