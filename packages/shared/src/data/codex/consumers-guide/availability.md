## Availability

All trappings have one of four Availability ratings:

| Availability | Village | Town | City |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Common** | In Stock! | In Stock! | In Stock! |
| **Scarce** | 30% | 60% | 90% |
| **Rare** | 15% | 30% | 45% |
| **Exotic** | Not in Stock | Not in Stock | Not in Stock |

On a failed Availability Test for Scarce or Rare items, you may reroll when reaching a new settlement, or try again next week if in a Town or larger. Exotic items are only available if the GM permits, or through a **[Commission](/codex/endeavours/commission)** or **[Crafting](/codex/endeavours/crafting)** Endeavour.

**Stock quantities (general guideline):**
- Villages: 1 item.
- Towns: 1d10 items.
- Cities: As many as the GM deems appropriate.

Common items are generally doubled; Rare items are halved (round up).

> **Tip:** Availability can be increased by +10–20% if a character is especially diligent, belongs to a relevant Career (Merchant, Fence), or spends an entire day shopping combined with **[Gossip](/codex/skills/gossip)** Tests.

> **Optional Rule — Guilders:** With an appropriate Guild Licence, you may make a **[Gossip](/codex/skills/gossip)** Test when you fail an Availability Test. On success, a fellow Guilder points you toward a source and you may reroll the Availability Test.
