## Money

The Empire's currency uses three denominations:

| Denomination | Abbreviation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Gold Crown | GC | 20/– = 240d |
| Silver Shilling | /– | 12d |
| Brass Penny | d | — |

**Standard conversions:**
- 1 GC = 20 silver shillings = 240 brass pennies
- 1 silver shilling = 12 brass pennies

> **Money Slang:** Gold Crowns are called *Marks*, *Karls*, or *Gelt*. Silver Shillings go by *Bob*, *Shimmies*, or *Mucks*. Brass Pennies are *Pfennigs*, *Clanks*, or *Shrapnel*.

A common coin worth 80d (one third of a Gold Crown) is called a **Noble**.
