## Food, Drink, and Lodging

Poor quality costs **half** the listed price; fine quality costs **double**. Poor quality food or drink carries a **10% chance** of contracting the [Galloping Trots](/codex/mechanics/disease-and-infection).

**Ale, keg:** Capacity 3 gallons. Empty kegs can be refilled for 18d. **Bugman's XXXXXX Ale:** Legendary Dwarfen brew from Josef Bugman's renowned brewery. One mug counts as 4 mugs of normal ale for intoxication purposes, and grants **immunity to Fear Tests** for 1d10 hours. **Room, Common:** Guests should be wary of thieves. **Room, Inn:** Accommodates 2; large rooms (double price) accommodate 4.
