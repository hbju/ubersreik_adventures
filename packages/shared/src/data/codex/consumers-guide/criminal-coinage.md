## Criminal Coinage

**Counterfeiting**
The diversity of coinage across the Empire makes counterfeiting rife. A successful **[Evaluate](/codex/skills/evaluate)** Test detects fake coins by inspecting weight and hardness. Producing counterfeits requires an **[Art](/codex/skills/art) (Engraving)** Test to carve a convincing die, then a **[Trade](/codex/skills/trade) (Blacksmith)** Test to strike the coins. A higher precious metal content makes detection harder; less than one fifth makes it far easier to spot.

**Clipping**
Embezzlers trim slivers of precious metal from coin edges — a practice called *clipping* or *shaving*, favoured by shopkeepers and tollkeepers with access to large quantities of others' coins. The filings are sold to jewellers, counterfeiters, or fences. Clipped coins can be detected with the **[Evaluate](/codex/skills/evaluate)** Skill; the more metal removed, the easier the Test.

> **The Nuln Standard:** Coin weight and metallurgy are governed by the standards established in Nuln — the Empire's former capital. Stamp imagery varies considerably by province and is not regulated.
