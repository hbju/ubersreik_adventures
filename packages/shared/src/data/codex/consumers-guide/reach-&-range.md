## Weapon Reach

From shortest to longest:

| Reach | Length | Special |
|---|---|---|
| Personal | Unarmed — limbs only | — |
| Very Short | Less than 1 foot | — |
| Short | Up to 2 feet | — |
| Average | Up to 3 feet | — |
| Long | Up to 6 feet | — |
| Very Long | Up to 10 feet | Can Engage enemies up to 4 yards away |
| Massive | Over 10 feet | Can Engage enemies up to 6 yards away |

> **Optional Rule — Weapon Length and In-Fighting:**
> **Length advantage:** If your weapon is longer than your opponent's, they suffer **–10 to hit** you.
> **In-Fighting:** As your Action, make an **[Opposed](/codex/mechanics/opposed-tests) [Melee Test](/codex/skills/melee)** to step inside your opponent's reach. The winner decides whether combat continues normally or as in-fighting. During in-fighting, any weapon longer than **Short** counts as an Improvised Weapon.

## Weapon Range Bands

A weapon's listed range is its **medium range** in yards. Other bands are calculated as follows:

| Range Band | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Point Blank | Range ÷ 10 |
| Short | Range ÷ 2 |
| Medium (listed) | As listed |
| Long | Range × 2 |
| Extreme | Range × 3 |

#### Sample Range Band Reference

| Weapon | Point Blank | Short | Medium | Long | Extreme |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bow | 5 yds | 25 yds | 50 yds | 100 yds | 150 yds |
| Heavy Crossbow | 10 yds | 50 yds | 100 yds | 200 yds | 300 yds |
| Pistol | 2 yds | 10 yds | 20 yds | 40 yds | 60 yds |
| Sling | 6 yds | 30 yds | 60 yds | 120 yds | 180 yds |
