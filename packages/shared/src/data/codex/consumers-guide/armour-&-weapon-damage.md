## Weapon Damage

Each point of Damage a weapon sustains (from Fumbles, spells, or special abilities) reduces its Damage by **–1**. If reduced to **+0** (or **SB +0**), the weapon is mangled and counts as an **Improvised Weapon**. A damaged Improvised Weapon is useless in melee.

Repairs cost **10% of the weapon's value per Damage point**. Weapons reduced to Improvised cannot be repaired. You may repair your own weapons with the appropriate **[Trade Skill](/codex/skills/trade)**, **Trade Tools**, and (for more than 1 Damage) a **Workshop**.

## Armour Damage

Whenever instructed to damage a piece of armour, reduce its **AP** in the struck location by **1**. APs reduced to 0 render the armour in that location useless.

Armour is damaged by:
1. A special ability (spell, Talent, etc.) triggering damage.
2. **Critical Deflection:** If you suffer a **[Critical Wound](/codex/mechanics/critical-wounds)** on an armoured location, you may choose to let your armour absorb it — the armour loses **1 AP** and the Critical Wound effects are ignored. You still suffer all normal Wounds (and likely 1 extra Wound since AP is now reduced).

**Repairing Armour:** Costs **10% of the armour's base price per AP lost**. A completely destroyed armour section costs **30%** to repair and takes significant time. You may repair your own armour with the appropriate **[Trade Skill](/codex/skills/trade)**, **Trade Tools**, and (for Plate Armour) a **Workshop**.
