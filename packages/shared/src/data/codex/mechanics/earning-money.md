## Earning Money

Status is directly linked to **how much money you earn** during play. Spending a week working in your Career (where feasible) is called **Earning**.

Make an **Average (+20) Dramatic Test** against your **[Earning Skill](/codex/mechanics/class-&-career)** (the italicised Skill in your Career's first level):

| Result | Income |
|---|---|
| Success | Full income (see table below) |
| Failure | Half income |
| Astounding Failure (–6 SL) | No income (or money stolen, or similar mishap) |

### Earning Income by Tier

| Tier | Amount Earned per Standing |
|---|---|
| Brass | 2d10 Brass Pennies per Standing |
| Silver | 1d10 Silver Shillings per Standing |
| Gold | 1 Gold Crown per Standing |

> **Note:** This is the same amount earned via the **[Income Endeavour](/codex/endeavours/income)**.

The income total represents money **remaining after weekly expenses** — not raw earnings.

> **Example:** Gunther the Coachman (Status: Silver 2) spends a week working at the Four Seasons Coaching House. He rolls against **[Drive](/codex/skills/drive) (Coach)** (his Earning Skill) and passes. He rolls 2d10 for his income, scoring a 3 — only 3 silver shillings remaining after a week of long shifts and after-work drinking.
