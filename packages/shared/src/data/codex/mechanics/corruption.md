## Corruption

Corruption represents the slow erosion of a soul toward Chaos. Corruption Points accumulate through exposure to dark influences, and trigger mutation when they exceed a threshold.

### Gaining Corruption Points

**Dark Deals:** When a Test is critical and all Fortune has been spent, you may voluntarily take **1 Corruption Point** to reroll a Test (even one already rerolled).

**Corrupting Influences:** On encountering a corrupting source, make a **Challenging (+0) [Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** or **[Cool](/codex/skills/cool)** Test as directed by the GM.

| Exposure Level | Condition | Effect on Failure |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor** | Witnessing a Lesser Daemon, contact with a Mutant, warpstone artefact; giving in to despair/rage/excess | **+1 Corruption Point** on any failure |
| **Moderate** | Multiple Daemons; contact with a Daemon or Warpstone; prolonged exposure to Dhar | **+2 CP** on failure; **+1 CP** on Marginal Success (0–1 SL); **+0** on Success (2+ SL) |
| **Major** | Greater Daemon; prolonged contact with Daemon/Warpstone; making a deal with a Daemon; consuming Warpstone | **+3 CP** on failure; **+2 CP** on Marginal Success; **+1 CP** on Success (2–3 SL); **+0** only on Impressive Success (4+ SL) |

### Triggering Mutation

When Corruption Points exceed **Willpower Bonus + Toughness Bonus**, make a **Challenging (+0) [Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test. Failure triggers mutation:

1. Lose Corruption Points equal to **Willpower Bonus**.
2. Roll percentile dice and consult the racial table below to determine Body or Mind mutation.

| Race | Body | Mind |
|---|---|---|
| Elf | — | 01–100 |
| Halfling | 01–10 | 11–100 |
| Human | 01–50 | 51–100 |
| Dwarf | 01–05 | 06–100 |

### Corruption Limits

Accumulate more **physical mutations** than your **Toughness Bonus**, or more **mental corruptions** than your **Willpower Bonus** — your character has fallen entirely to Chaos and becomes an NPC.

### Losing Corruption Points

**Dark Whispers:** The GM may spend 1 of your Corruption Points, asking you to perform a minor act of chaos (letting an enemy escape, shooting an ally, sleeping on watch). You may refuse, keeping the Corruption Point.

**Absolution:** A significant act of piety or heroism guided by the GM. Examples:
- Cleansing a profane temple (at risk of further corruption).
- Completing a dangerous holy pilgrimage.
- Destroying an unholy artefact.
- Joining a holy order dedicated to opposing Chaos.
