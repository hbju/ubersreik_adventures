## Fate & Resilience

### Fate and Fortune

**Fate** marks a character's destiny. Characters begin with a number of **Fate Points** that define their maximum **Fortune** pool.

**Fortune** points replenish at the start of each session (up to your current Fate total).

#### Spending Fortune

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| Reroll | Reroll a failed Test (once per Test). |
| +1 SL | Add **+1 SL** to any Test after it is rolled. |
| Act First | At the start of the Round, choose when to act, ignoring Initiative order. |

#### Spending Fate (Permanent)

Permanently reduces your Fate and Fortune maximum. Used to avoid death:

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Die Another Day** | Instead of dying, your character is knocked out and removed from the current encounter. Survives, but takes no further part. |
| **How Did That Miss?** | Your character completely avoids the incoming damage through an extraordinary fluke and may continue fighting — but remains in danger. |

The GM narrates how you survive. A **Fate Point** may be granted for an act of extreme heroism at the end of a major adventure.
