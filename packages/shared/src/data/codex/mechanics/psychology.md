## Psychology

Powerful emotional states can override rational behaviour. When exposed to a Psychological Trait, make a **[Cool](/codex/skills/cool)** Test at the GM's chosen Difficulty at the start of the Round. Success lasts the encounter (unless circumstances change); further Tests may be called for.

### Animosity (Target)

You harbour deep resentment for a group. When encountering them, Test **[Cool](/codex/skills/cool)**:
- **Pass:** –20 to all Fellowship Tests toward the group.
- **Fail:** Subject to Animosity; must immediately attack the group (socially or physically) and gain **+1 SL** on attempts to attack them socially or mentally.

Re-test at end of each Round. Ends naturally when the group is gone, pacified, or you gain [Stunned](/codex/condition/condition_stunned) or [Unconscious](/codex/condition/condition_unconscious).

*Animosity is overridden by Fear and Terror.*

### Fear (Rating)

An extreme aversion. Make an Extended **[Cool](/codex/skills/cool)** Test until SL meets or exceeds the creature's **Fear Rating**. Until then:
- **–1 SL** on all Tests to affect the source of fear.
- Cannot voluntarily move closer without a **Challenging (+0) [Cool](/codex/skills/cool)** Test.
- If the source moves closer: **Challenging (+0) [Cool](/codex/skills/cool)** Test or gain [Broken](/codex/condition/condition_broken).

### Frenzy

Voluntarily enter a Frenzy with a **Willpower** Test. While Frenzied:
- Immune to all other Psychology; will never flee.
- Must move at full speed toward the nearest visible enemy and attack.
- May only take Weapon Skill Tests or **[Athletics](/codex/skills/athletics)** Tests to close distance.
- Gain a **free Melee Test** each Round (in addition to normal Action).
- **+1 Strength Bonus** from ferocity.

Ends when all visible enemies are pacified or you gain [Stunned](/codex/condition/condition_stunned)/[Unconscious](/codex/condition/condition_unconscious). Gain 1 [Fatigued](/codex/condition/condition_fatigued) Condition on recovery.

### Hatred (Target)

Consuming hatred of a group. Will never interact socially with them.
- **Fail Psychology Test:** Must immediately attempt to destroy the hated group by the most direct and lethal means available.
- **+1 SL** on all combat Tests against the hated group.
- Immune to **Fear** and **Intimidate** (but not **Terror**) from the hated group.

Ends when all visible members are dead or gone, or you gain [Unconscious](/codex/condition/condition_unconscious).

### Prejudice (Target)

Strong dislike of a group.
- **Pass:** –10 to all Fellowship Tests toward the group.
- **Fail:** Must immediately insult the target — loudly.

Re-test at end of each Round.

### Terror (Rating)

Bone-chilling dread. Make a **[Cool](/codex/skills/cool)** Test on first encounter:
- **Pass:** No further effect.
- **Fail:** Gain **[Broken](/codex/condition/condition_broken)** Conditions equal to the creature's **Terror Rating** + SL below 0.

After the initial Terror Test, the creature then causes **Fear** at a rating equal to its Terror Rating.

### Custom Psychology

GMs and players may create custom Psychological Traits. Define:
- What triggers the trait.
- What effects it produces (bonus/penalty SL, immunity to specific Psychology, mandatory Actions, Conditions gained, etc.).

> **Example — Camaraderie:** You feel protective kinship toward a group. Must aid them if threatened. +1 SL to Tests defending or supporting them.
>
> **Example — Love:** Deep emotional connection to a specific individual or individuals. Immune to **Fear** and **Intimidation** while defending them. +1 SL on related Tests. Must come to their aid.
>
> **Example — Phobia:** Specific fear of an object or circumstance (e.g., Phobia (Snakes)). Treat the source as causing **Fear 1** (or higher for powerful phobias).
>
> **Example — Trauma:** Manifests as nightmares, Animosity, Hatred, Phobia, or other effects tied to a specific past event. Example: Horst's village burned — seeing any [Ablaze](/codex/condition/condition_ablaze) character triggers a **Challenging (+0) [Cool](/codex/skills/cool)** Test or gain [Stunned](/codex/condition/condition_stunned) ×1 per SL failed.
