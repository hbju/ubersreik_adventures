## Tests

At the core of WFRP's rules are **Tests** — dice rolls that determine the outcome of uncertain actions. The GM decides when a Test is required; sometimes they will simply narrate a result based on a character's abilities, but when stakes are high or failure has significant consequences, the dice are rolled.

### The Golden Rule

> The rules exist to help you enjoy epic stories in the Warhammer world. If a rule negatively impacts fun for your group, change it or ignore it.

### Resolving Actions

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Outcome is uncertain but not dramatically important | GM decides, or **Simple Test** |
| Success/failure is dramatic or story-significant | **Dramatic Test** |
| Degree of success/failure matters | **Dramatic Test** |
| Two characters are directly opposing each other | **[Opposed Test](/codex/mechanics/opposed-tests)** |
| A task requires accumulating multiple successes | **[Extended Test](/codex/mechanics/extended-tests)** |

## Characteristic Tests

When no Skill applies, the GM may call for a **Characteristic Test** instead — roll against the most relevant Characteristic directly, handling it exactly like a Skill Test.
