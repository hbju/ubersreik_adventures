## Disease and Infection

Diseases are defined by:

- **Contraction** — How the disease is acquired (usually an Endurance or Toughness Test).
- **Incubation** — Time before symptoms appear.
- **Duration** — How long symptoms last untreated.
- **Symptoms** — Ongoing mechanical effects (see Symptoms below).
- **Permanent** — Some diseases leave lasting consequences even after recovery.
