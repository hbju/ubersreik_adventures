## Disease Symptoms

### Blight
Risk of death. Each day, pass an **[Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test or die:
- Standard Blight: Very Easy (+60).
- **Moderate:** Easy (+40).
- **Severe:** Average (+20).

*Treatment: None.*

### Buboes
Painful lymph node swellings. **–10** to all physical and (if visible) Fellowship Tests.

*Treatment:* Successful **[Heal](/codex/skills/heal)** with **Surgery** lances them. Failure: contract **Festering Wound**. Even if lanced, make a **Difficult (–10) [Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test daily or they return.

### Convulsions
Periodic spasms. **–10** to all physical Tests.
- **(Moderate):** –20.
- **(Severe):** Must be restrained; effectively incapacitated.

*Treatment:* Rare compounds (Challenging **Trade (Apothecary)** skill; ~1 GC per dose from apothecaries) reduce Severe → Moderate → Standard for a day.

### Coughs and Sneezes
Infectious. All nearby characters must Test for Contraction each hour of exposure.

*Treatment: None.*

### Fever
High temperature; suffering. **–10** to all physical and Fellowship Tests.
- **(Severe):** [Unconscious](/codex/condition/condition_unconscious); Resolve points can bring brief awareness.

*Treatment:* Commercial cures are only 10% genuine. Genuine cures: pass a **Challenging (+0) [Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test to remove standard Fever (not Severe).

### Flux
Urgent and uncontrollable bowel episodes. The GM may trigger an episode once per session (Moderate: twice; Severe: three times). You have a number of Rounds equal to your **Toughness Bonus** to reach a privy; your body will act regardless.
- **(Severe):** Also lose 1 Wound per episode.

*Treatment:* Only 10% of commercial remedies work. Genuine ones hold off symptoms for **Toughness Bonus hours**.

### Gangrene
Rotting flesh. Roll for Hit Location; Body = no Gangrene settled. Otherwise:
- Each day, pass an **Average (+20) [Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test or the Gangrene worsens.
- Fail more times than your **Toughness Bonus**: location is entirely lost (Amputated Parts rules apply).
- Suffer **–10** Fellowship and the **Wounded** symptom. Also contract **Blight** until affected tissue is amputated.

*Treatment:* Amputation only.

### Lingering
The disease refuses to end. After the normal Duration, pass an **[Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test at the listed Difficulty:
- Marginal Failure (0): Duration extended by 1d10 days.
- Failure (–2): Develop a **Festering Wound**.
- Astounding Failure (–6): Develop **Blood Rot**.

*Treatment:* Genuine cures (10% chance) negate the Test if taken on the correct day (requires a successful [Heal](/codex/skills/heal) Test to determine timing).

### Malaise
Fatigue and inability to concentrate. Gain a [Fatigued](/codex/condition/condition_fatigued) Condition that cannot be removed until recovered.

*Treatment:* 75% of commercial remedies work. Successful **Challenging (+0) [Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test to ignore for the day.

### Nausea
Nausea and vomiting risk. Failing any physical Test triggers vomiting and the [Stunned](/codex/condition/condition_stunned) Condition.

*Treatment:* 60% of remedies are genuine. Genuine: pass **Challenging (+0) [Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test to suppress Nausea for **Toughness Bonus hours**.

### Pox
Pustules and itchy rash. **–10** to Fellowship Tests. Pass an **Average (+20) [Cool](/codex/skills/cool)** Test to resist scratching.

After recovery: pass an **Average (+20) [Cool](/codex/skills/cool)** Test or gain permanent scarring at the affected Hit Location (Head = permanent –1 Fellowship).

*Treatment:* Creams/oils widely available (90% genuine; ~6–7 pence per week). Genuine creams raise all scratching Cool Tests to Very Easy (+60).

### Wounded
An infected wound that won't heal. Per Wounded symptom, 1 Wound cannot be healed naturally. Each day, pass an **Easy (+20) [Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test or develop a **Festering Wound** (if not already present).

*Treatment:* Daily successful **[Heal](/codex/skills/heal)** Test keeps the wound clean and negates the Endurance Test.
