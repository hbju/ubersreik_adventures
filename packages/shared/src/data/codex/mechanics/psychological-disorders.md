# Psychological Disorders

Life in the Old World leaves its mark. Characters can gain new Psychologies through play — and making them dramatic earns **Motivation XP**.

---

## Fear of the Dark

Whenever a Character suffers **Terror**, record the number of Broken Conditions suffered. When the total equals or exceeds their current **Willpower Characteristic**, the Character gains a **Phobia** related to the most recent or most common source of Terror (mirrors, leeches, Sylvanian accents, etc.).

Once the Phobia is gained, the Broken Condition tally **resets to zero**.

---

## Animosity and Hatred

Whenever a Character **spends a Fate point to avoid death**, they must make a **Challenging (+0) Cool Test**. On a failure, they gain the **Animosity** Psychology toward whatever almost killed them.

- If the target is the **same species** as the Character, the Animosity is specific (e.g. *Reiklanders*, *Watchmen*, *Imperial Dwarfs*), not the whole species.
- If the target is a **different species**, the Animosity applies to that entire species.
- If the Character **already has Animosity** toward the same target, it becomes **Hatred**.

---

## Trauma

A traumatic event — the death of a loved one, a stunning failure, a terrible regret — can become a defining incident. If a Character witnesses an event that **renders one of their Ambitions entirely impossible**, they must make an **Average (+20) Cool Test**. On a failure, they gain a **Trauma Psychology**.

> **Example:** *Deiter's Short-Term Ambition is to avenge the death of his comrade Hans. Before he can, Hans's killer is caught and hanged for an unrelated crime. Deiter fails the Cool Test and gains a Trauma — he becomes unwilling to enter any town square where a gallows stands.*

---

## Things Get Better

The GM is encouraged to **reward good roleplay** with the opportunity to remove a Psychology, especially if it has already played its part in the story.

Additionally, any time a Character **would recover a point of Resilience**, they may instead choose to **remove an unwanted Psychology**.

---

## New Endeavour: Recuperation

*Available at The Great Hospice, a monastery, or similar place of rest.*

You make a concerted effort at rest and self-reflection. Make a **Very Hard (–30) Cool Test**. The difficulty may be reduced depending on the quality of care received.

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| **Success** | Remove one chosen Psychology. |
| **Failure** | You may reverse one Cool Test made to resist the effects of Psychology during your next adventure. |
