## Encumbrance

The number of Encumbrance points you can carry without penalty = **[Strength](/codex/characteristics/strength) Bonus + [Toughness](/codex/characteristics/toughness) Bonus** (average humans begin with ~6 Enc).

| Enc | Example Trappings |
|---|---|
| 0 | Knives, Coins, Jewellery |
| 1 | Sword, Mandolin, Sling Bag |
| 2 | Greatsword, Tent, Backpack |
| 3 | Halberd, Cask, Large Sack |

**Small Items:** Money weighs 1 Enc per 200 coins. Common sense governs small item stacking.

**Oversized Items:** Items weighing 4+ Enc (barrels, saddlebags) — normally only one may be carried, requiring both hands.

**Beasts of Burden:** Draft animals ignore the **SB + TB** formula; their Enc capacity is listed in their descriptions. Each human-sized passenger weighs approximately 10 Enc.

**Worn Items:** Worn armour, clothing, and jewellery have their Encumbrance reduced by 1 (often to 0).

### Overburdened

| Enc Carried | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Up to limit | No penalties |
| Up to double limit | –1 Movement (min 3), –10 Agility, +1 Travel Fatigue |
| Up to triple limit | –2 Movement (min 2), –20 Agility (min 10), +2 Travel Fatigue |
| More than 3× limit | You are not moving |

Movement penalties apply immediately and are only removed by dropping equipment. 
> Travel Fatigue : **[Fatigued](/codex/conditions/fatigued)** Conditions from Encumbrance can only be removed with a long rest, and stack with armour penalties.
