## Resilience and Resolve

**Resilience** represents personal determination. Your **Resolve** pool is capped at your current **Resilience** total and replenishes when you act in accordance with your **Motivation**.

### Spending Resolve

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| Immune to Psychology | Become immune to Psychology until the end of the next Round. |
| Ignore Critical Wound Modifiers | Ignore all modifiers from Critical Wounds until the start of your next Turn. |
| Remove a Condition | Remove one Condition. If [Prone](/codex/condition/condition_prone) is removed this way, also regain 1 Wound. |

### Spending Resilience (Permanent)

Permanently reduces your Resilience and Resolve maximum.

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| **I Deny You!** | Choose not to develop a rolled mutation; you also do not lose Corruption points. |
| **I Will Not Fail!** | Choose the result of a Test instead of rolling. In an Opposed Test, you always win by at least **+1 SL**. Can be invoked even after a Test has already failed. |

> **Example:** A bandit leader has built up 10 Advantage. Salundra loses an Opposed Test by **–7 SL** — a devastating blow incoming. She spends a permanent Resilience point on **I Will Not Fail!**, wins by **+1 SL**, chooses a roll of 11 to trigger a Critical, and the bandit loses all 10 Advantage.

A **Resilience Point** may be granted by the GM for an act of extreme significance to a character's Motivation.
