## Conditions

Conditions represent ongoing physical or mental states. They are tracked with tokens, chits, or a written tally.

> **Note:** Suffering any Condition causes **immediate loss of all [Advantage](/codex/mechanics/advantage)**.

### Multiple Conditions

You may hold the **same Condition multiple times**; penalties **stack**. When holding **different** Conditions simultaneously, the penalties **do not stack** — apply only the **highest penalty**.

> **Exception:** [Prone](/codex/condition/condition_prone) does not stack.

A Condition can always be removed by spending a **Resolve** point.
