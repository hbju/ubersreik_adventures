## Death

Death can result from Critical Damage directly, or from accumulated injuries. If a character has the **[Unconscious](/codex/conditions/unconscious) Condition** and **0 Wounds**, total all their current **Critical Wounds** (results from Critical Damage tables — see Trivial Injuries below for exceptions). If this total **exceeds their Toughness Bonus**, they die at the end of the Round unless healed of at least 1 Critical Wound.

Furthermore, any enemy with a suitable weapon may **kill an Unconscious character** by spending an Action to do so.
