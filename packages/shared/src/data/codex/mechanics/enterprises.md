# Enterprises

Enterprises are business ventures Characters can invest in over the course of weeks, months, or years. They offer income beyond what a Career's Earning Skill provides, unique special rules, and the potential for adventure.

**No Character can manage more than one Enterprise at once.**

---

## Starting an Enterprise

Pay the **Start-Up Costs** listed in the chosen Enterprise template. If you cannot afford the full cost, you may approach a **Creditor** — but you must pay at least **10%** of Start-Up Costs yourself to attract one.

Acquiring an Enterprise costs **one Endeavour**.

---

## Creditors

Creditors are NPCs who loan the remaining Start-Up Costs in exchange for ongoing **Interest Payments**. They are avaricious, unsympathetic, and well-connected. They do not reduce your Debt — they only collect interest until you pay them off.

### Repaying Debt

Spend an Endeavour to seek out your Creditor and pay off some or all of your outstanding Debt. There is no time limit, but **an Enterprise in Debt cannot Expand**.

### Interest Payments

At the end of each downtime period (after Events and Endeavours, before unspent money is lost), pay the amount listed in the template. This **does not** require an Endeavour.

### Missing Interest Payments

| Missed Payments | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **1st miss** | A Conversation. The Creditor demands an explanation. Penitent Characters may avoid consequences — but the next payment is **triple**. Alternatively, owe a **Minor Favour**. |
| **2nd miss** | A Warning. Debt collectors seize Trappings, politicians obstruct you, or brutes break your legs. Could happen immediately or during the next adventure. Alternatively, owe a **Major Favour**. |
| **3rd miss** | An Example. Debtor's prison, fugitive status, or a very public hit-squad. The Enterprise is seized or burned. Alternatively, owe a **Significant Favour**. |

---

## Expansion

Once **all Debt is repaid**, the Enterprise may Expand. Expansion costs an Endeavour and an infusion of cash (returning you to Debt). Each template has **three Expansion levels**, each unlocking new Trappings, improved Status, and special rules.

---

## Enterprise Events

Between adventures, Characters may choose to roll on the **Enterprise Events Table** instead of the standard Events Table. Only **one** Character per Enterprise may do this per downtime phase.

See **[Enterprise Events](/codex/enterprises/enterprise-events)** for the full table.

## Template Summaries

### Courier Service
Postal runs, river ferries, express deliveries. **Special Rule:** Using the Enterprise for Income gives +20 (or +40 for a small territory) to travel and navigation Tests during the next adventure.

### Crafting Workshop
Artisan production and commissioned items. **Special Rule:** Choose one Art or Trade specialisation — Tests using that Skill within the workshop gain +20. If a Trade Skill is chosen, raw materials for Crafting Endeavours are free (not for Inventing).

### Criminal Gang
Theft, extortion, contraband, murder-for-hire. **Special Rule:** Characters may borrow all Start-Up Costs from a Creditor with no personal contribution. Any Character who invests at least 1/- gains the **Criminal** Talent for free (once only).

### Holy Temple
Shrine, monastery, sacred site. **Special Rule:** Choose a patron deity — Characters entreating that deity within the temple grounds gain +20 to Pray Tests and may treat Pray as a Basic Skill. All investors may use **Holy Visions** while on the grounds.

### Knightly Order
Chapterhouse, secular or templar order. **Special Rule:** Investors of at least 1 GC gain +20 to Leadership Tests targeting loyal Imperial citizens. Investors in a secular order add **Warrior Born** to available Talents; templar order investors add **Holy Visions**.

### Market Parlour
General or specialist goods stall or shop. **Special Rule:** Income from general goods grants +20 to Haggle Tests until end of next adventure. Income from specialist goods grants +20 to Availability Tests.

### Noble Estate
Farm, lodge, manor wing, or estate parcel. **Special Rule:** Investors of at least 6p **double the bonuses and penalties of Status** on all Skill Tests they undertake.

### Performance Troupe
Circus, theatre, band of bards. **Special Rule:** Investors may treat all Perform and Play Skills as Basic Skills, or add one Entertain/Perform/Play specialisation to their Career Skills. Spending an Endeavour to produce new material increases the next three Income Endeavours by +50%.

### Publishing House
Printing press, newspaper, academic journal. **Special Rule:** First Income Endeavour grants the **Read/Write** Talent (or **Speedreader** if already possessed). Investors of at least 1/- gain +20 to Research Lore Endeavour Tests.

### Tavern
Pub, inn, hostel. **Special Rule:** Regulars add Consume Alcohol to their Career Skills. Any Character beginning an adventure at the Tavern may choose to start **Stinking Drunk** (roll 1d10, see WFRP p.121) and gain a **free Fortune point** (may exceed maximum — resets at next session's start).
