## Assistance

With the GM's permission, one character may assist another on a Test. The **character with the best skill rolls**, and each assisting character provides **+10** to the Test.

**Limits on Assistance:**
- Assisting characters must have at least **1 Advance** in the tested Skill.
- Assistants must normally be **adjacent** to the character testing.
- Cannot assist on Tests to resist **disease, poison, fear, or hazards**.
- Maximum assistants = the character's relevant **Characteristic Bonus**.

> **Example:** Adhemar, Perdita, and Valentyn search a room. The GM calls for a **Difficult (–10) Perception** Test. Adhemar has the highest **Perception** at 59. With two assistants (+20), he rolls against **69** (59–10+20). He rolls 74 — failure.
