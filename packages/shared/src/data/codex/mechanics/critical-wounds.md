## Critical Wounds

When a Critical Wound is received, roll a fresh **1d100** for Hit Location, then roll another **1d100** and consult the relevant **Critical Table** below. Wounds inflicted by Critical Tables are **not** reduced by Toughness Bonus or Armour Points.

If [Unconscious](/codex/condition/condition_unconscious), the character remains unconscious until the encounter ends or they receive **Medical Attention**, unless the result states otherwise.
