## Critical Wounds

### Generating a Critical Hit

There are two ways to inflict a Critical Wound:

**1. Doubles on a successful attack.** If a character rolls **a double** on their Melee or Ranged Test (or WS/BS if not using a Skill) and the Test **succeeds**, they inflict Critical Damage — even if the opponent still has Wounds remaining.

**2. Any damage after 0 Wounds.** If the target has already been reduced to 0 Wounds, any successful hit automatically inflicts Critical Damage.

---

### Resolving a Critical Hit

**Do not reverse your attack roll** to determine Hit Location as you would for a normal hit. Instead:

1. **Roll d100 again** to determine the **Hit Location** (using the standard Hit Location table).
2. **Roll d100 again** to determine the **result** on the relevant Critical Wound table for that location.

**If the opponent has 0 Wounds**, add **+10 to the Critical Wound table roll** for every Wound inflicted **beyond** what was needed to reduce the target to 0 Wounds.

> **Example:** An opponent already at 0 Wounds is hit for 8 Wounds. Add +80 to the d100 roll on the Critical Damage table.

**Additional Wound Loss from Critical Damage:** The Wounds column on each Critical Wound table indicates extra Wounds lost as a result of the Critical. Calculate these **after** all other effects of the hit. Wounds from this column do not themselves trigger further Critical Wound rolls. If a hit brings an opponent below 0 Wounds, it does not cause a second table roll — **each blow can only inflict a single Critical Wound**.

---

### Trivial Injuries

Not all Critical Damage is life-threatening. Results marked with **T** in the Wounds column are **Trivial** — they inflict no extra Wounds and do **not count** toward the Critical Wound total for determining death.

---

> **Optional Rule — Sudden Death:** The GM may bypass injury tracking for background NPCs by simply declaring them dead or unconscious when they reach 0 Wounds. This should **not** be used for Player Characters or NPCs with the **Luck** Talent.
