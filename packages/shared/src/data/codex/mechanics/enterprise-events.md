# Enterprise Events Table

Roll instead of the standard Events Table between adventures. Only **one** Character per Enterprise may roll here per downtime phase. Results 55–57 and 58–60 are replaced by the **Alternate Events** listed in each Enterprise's own template.

| Roll | Event | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 01–02 | **Looming Bankruptcy** | If the Enterprise has Expanded, lose all benefits from the most recent Expansion (but keep the Debt and Interest). Only repaying all Debt restores them. If never Expanded, the business closes — all staff laid off, Trappings sold to cover costs, Debts remain. The GM may offer one last desperate scheme as the next adventure. |
| 03–05 | **Recession** | No Character may use the Enterprise for Earning or Income Endeavours until the end of the next adventure. |
| 06–08 | **Shoplifter Strikes** | The most valuable portable Trapping is stolen. Attempt to reclaim it next adventure, or accept the loss. |
| 09–10 | **Interest Inflation** | The Creditor invokes an obscure clause. Interest Payments are **doubled** until all Debt is paid off. |
| 11–13 | **Trapping Degrades** | One Trapping deteriorates — an item gains a Flaw, a trained animal loses a Skill, or an employee gains a Condition. Severe problems are usually temporary; milder ones persist. |
| 14–16 | **New Competition** | A rival enterprise muscles in. Must undertake at least one Income Endeavour this round, but receive only **50%** of usual income. The rival becomes an ongoing threat. |
| 17–20 | **Forced to Diversify** | The GM chooses the most commonly used Source of Income — it is unavailable this round of Endeavours due to a supply or demand drop. Others function normally. |
| 21–23 | **The Dreaded Auditors** | Spend an Endeavour assisting the audit, or pay **10%** of the Enterprise's most recent Start-Up or Expansion Cost in non-compliance fees. |
| 24–26 | **Gimme a Discount** | A customer demands a substantial discount. Refuse: they slander the business publicly. Acquiesce: other customers follow and Income Statuses may drop. Decide now, face consequences next adventure. |
| 27–30 | **I Need a Holiday** | Severe burnout. You cannot choose the Income Endeavour this round. Other investors may use the Enterprise as normal. |
| 31–33 | **Conservative Investments** | No Characters may acquire a Creditor to Expand an Enterprise this round of Endeavours. |
| 34–36 | **The Grind** | Must choose an Income Endeavour before the next adventure, if possible. No other special effects. |
| 37–39 | **I Quit!** | An NPC staff member leaves due to dissatisfaction, ambition, or death. Replaced by start of next adventure — could be an opportunity to work with an established NPC contact. |
| 40–41 | **New Creditor** | The Creditor sells your Debt to someone else. The new Creditor is probably just as bad. |
| 42–44 | **Closed for Refurbishment** | Business accommodation Trappings are updated. Cannot collect Income this round. Afterwards, the accommodation may provide Skill Test bonuses at GM's discretion. |
| 45–48 | **Creditor Visits** | The Creditor pokes around the Enterprise. Before the next adventure, you may **repay Debt without using an Endeavour**. |
| 49–51 | **New Hire** | Recruit an NPC to help day-to-day. They are not particularly qualified, competent, or scrupulous — they will need supervision. |
| 52–54 | **Breakup Opportunity** | An investor or employee offers to buy a less-profitable fraction of the business. If accepted: lose all Trappings and benefits from the most recent Expansion, but pay off all outstanding Debt. You may Expand again later. |
| 55–57 | **Alternate Event 1** | See your specific Enterprise template. |
| 58–60 | **Alternate Event 2** | See your specific Enterprise template. |
| 61–63 | **Business as Usual** | Routine activities — taking stock, networking, keeping customers happy. No special effects. |
| 64–66 | **Co-Ownership** | An investor or employee offers to buy in as co-owner. If accepted: they pay off **half** of all outstanding Debt, but insist on having a say in how the Enterprise develops. |
| 67–69 | **Upgraded Trapping** | One Trapping improves — an item gains a Quality, a trained animal gains a Skill, an employee gains a new Skill, or a Flaw or Condition is resolved. |
| 70–72 | **Merger Opportunity** | Another business owner offers to join forces. You may immediately Expand the Enterprise. Expansion Costs must be paid normally, but **Expansion does not cost an Endeavour** and may be taken even if still in Debt. The new Debt and Interest rate take effect immediately. |
| 73–75 | **Desperate Customer** | A customer urgently needs your services and pays a premium. Choose one Source of Income — its effective Status increases by 1 until the start of the next adventure. |
| 76–78 | **In the Black** | The Enterprise is making a tidy profit. No special effects. |
| 79–81 | **Temporary Expansion** | A brief surge in demand. Until the end of the next adventure, the Enterprise enjoys all benefits of having Expanded one additional level. No Expansion Costs, Interest Payments unchanged. |
| 82–85 | **Taking Care of Itself** | The Enterprise runs itself for now. Immediately use the Enterprise for one Income Endeavour — this does **not** count toward your Endeavour limit. |
| 86–88 | **Innovation** | The Enterprise gains a new Source of Income. Work with the GM to define it. Effective Status should equal the second-highest existing Source of Income; the Earning Skill should be different from all current ones. |
| 89–91 | **Rolling Profits** | Business is booming. Choose one Source of Income and **permanently increase its effective Status by 1**. |
| 92–94 | **New Trapping** | Acquire a new Trapping with value equal to or less than the Enterprise's Start-Up or most recent Expansion Cost (whichever is higher). May be a duplicate of an existing Trapping. |
| 95–97 | **Unexpected Generosity** | The Creditor is in an unusually good mood. **No Interest Payment** required this period. |
| 98–100 | **Offer to Buy-Out** | A wealthy entrepreneur offers to purchase the Enterprise wholesale. If accepted: lose all Enterprise benefits, pay off all outstanding Debt, and share wealth equal to **double the Start-Up Costs or double the most recent Expansion Cost** (whichever is greater). |
