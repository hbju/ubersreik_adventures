## Injury

> **Variant Rule — Up in Arms:** This campaign uses the alternative injury and Critical Wound rules from *Up in Arms*. These replace the Critical Wound tables and Bleeding Condition rules in the core rulebook (WFRP pp. 172–177).

## Wounds and Damage

Standard damage is calculated as: **Weapon Damage + SL − Toughness Bonus − Armour Points = Wounds lost**.

> **Example:** Hit on the arm for 10 Damage, with TB 3 and 1 AP from leather armour: 10 – 3 – 1 = **6 Wounds**.


At **0 Wounds**, gain the [Prone](/codex/condition/condition_prone) Condition. If not healed within Toughness Bonus Rounds, also gain the [Unconscious](/codex/condition/condition_unconscious) Condition.

If Damage would reduce a character to **negative Wounds**, they instead suffer a **Critical Wound**. Characters with fewer negative Wounds than their Toughness Bonus subtract **–20** from the Critical Table roll (minimum 01). Characters never actually go into negative Wounds — the floor is always **0**.

### Death

While [Unconscious](/codex/condition/condition_unconscious) at 0 Wounds, compare total **Critical Wounds** to **Toughness Bonus**. If Critical Wounds exceed Toughness Bonus, the character will **die at the end of the Round** unless a Critical Wound is healed.

> **Optional Rule — Sudden Death:** When a target suffers more Damage than remaining Wounds, they simply die (or gain [Unconscious](/codex/condition/condition_unconscious) — GM's choice). Useful for minor enemies; should not apply to PCs or important NPCs.
