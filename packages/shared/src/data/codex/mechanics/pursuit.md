## Pursuit

Use this structured procedure for chase scenes:

1. **Determine Distance:** The GM assigns a numerical **Distance** representing the pursuer's head start (typically 1–8).
2. **Test:** All active participants roll an appropriate Test (**[Drive](/codex/skills/drive)**, **[Ride](/codex/skills/ride)**, or **[Athletics](/codex/skills/athletics)**).
3. **Update Distance:** Compare the **lowest SL of the pursued** to the **highest SL of the pursuers**. If the pursued win, add the difference to Distance; if pursuers win, subtract it.
4. **Determine Outcome:**
   - Distance **0 or less** → pursuers catch their quarry.
   - Distance **10+** → pursuers have lost the trail.
   - Distance **1–9** → return to Step 2.

**Movement Modifiers:** If a character has a higher **Movement** value than their opponent, they gain **+SL** equal to the difference on their pursuit roll.

> **Example:** Eichengard and Sigrid chase three cultists (Distance 2). Sigrid scores +3 SL; the cultists score +0, +2, +2 SL. The difference between the slowest cultist (0) and fastest pursuer (Sigrid, +3) is 3 — the pursuers close in. The cultists sacrifice their slowest to delay Sigrid, and the chase continues.

> **Optional Rule — Obstacles:** The highest-SL character each Round may create an Obstacle, forcing one named character to suffer **–1 SL** next Round.

> **Optional Rule — Environmental Tests:** The GM can vary the required Test each Round (Athletics one Round, Leadership to bluff a gate guard the next, etc.).
