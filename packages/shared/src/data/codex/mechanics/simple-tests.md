## Simple Tests

A **Simple Test** is used to determine whether a character succeeds or fails at a straightforward task — a quick yes-or-no answer.

Roll **1d100** and compare the result to the relevant **Skill** or **Characteristic**. If the roll is **equal to or lower**, the test is a **success**. If the roll is **higher**, it is a **failure**.

> **Example:** Molli crosses Altdorf's Königsplatz on an icy winter's night with thugs in pursuit. The GM calls for a Simple **[Athletics](/codex/skills/athletics)** Test. Molli's **Athletics** is 42. Her player rolls a 17 — lower than 42, so she crosses safely and ducks into an alleyway. A roll above 42 would have sent her sprawling into her pursuers' hands.

### Automatic Failure and Success

- Rolling **96–00** is **always a failure**, regardless of modified skill values.
- Rolling **01–05** is **always a success**, regardless of modified skill values.

> **Optional Rule — No Auto-Success:** Some groups dislike the 5% auto-success. The most common alternative is: **96–00 always fails**, and only **01 always succeeds**.
