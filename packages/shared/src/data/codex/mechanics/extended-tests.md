## Extended Tests

An **[Extended Test](/codex/mechanics/extended-tests)** requires a character to accumulate a specified number of **SL** across multiple rolls to complete a lengthy or taxing task.

Each roll adds or subtracts its SL from a running total. If the total ever drops below **0**, the character must start from scratch.

> **Example:** Molli must pick a complex lock requiring **5 SL** before a guard arrives in three rounds. Her **Pick Lock** skill is 58.
>
> - Round 1: Rolls 63 → **–1 SL**. Back to zero.
> - Round 2: Rolls 11 → **+4 SL**. Running total: 4.
> - Round 3 (last chance): Rolls 42 → **+1 SL**. Running total: **5**. The lock clicks open just as the guard rounds the corner.

> **Optional Rule — 0 SL in Extended Tests:** A successful roll always adds at least **+1 SL**; a failed roll always removes at least **–1 SL**.
