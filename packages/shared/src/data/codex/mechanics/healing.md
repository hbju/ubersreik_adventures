## Healing

### Healing Wounds

Without Medical Attention, attempt an **Average (+20) [Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test after a full night's sleep (once per day). On success, heal **SL + Toughness Bonus Wounds**. For each day of rest, also heal an extra number of Wounds equal to your **Toughness Bonus**.

Further healing requires the **[Heal](/codex/skills/heal)** Skill, bandages, poultices, or similar.

### Healing Critical Wounds

A Critical Wound is not considered healed until:
- All Conditions it inflicted are removed.
- All non-permanent negative modifiers are resolved.

Normal Wound healing may still be applied while Critically Wounded.

### Medical Attention

Several Critical Wound results require **Medical Attention** before they can be resolved. This includes a successful **[Heal](/codex/skills/heal)** Test, application of bandages or healing poultices, or a healing spell/prayer.

### Surgery

Some wounds require **Surgery**, noted explicitly in the Critical Tables. Until a surgeon (or magical/divine equivalent) treats it, the listed penalties persist. Amputated body parts also cannot have 1 Wound healed until Surgery is performed.
