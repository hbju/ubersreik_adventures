## Classes and Careers

> 'Well, I play a character with no class at all who would sell his grandmother for career advancement; you know, exactly the type of fellow frequently encountered in my line of work.'
> — Detlef Sierck, Playwright and Actor

Your **Career** is your job when not adventuring. It describes your training, social class, and future prospects. WFRP groups similar Careers into **Classes**.

### Classes

Classes organise Careers into easily referenced groups. They also impact character development, provide different starting Trappings during Character Creation, and influence how you can change Career. Some Classes have access to unique **[Endeavours](/codex/endeavours/endeavours)** between adventures.

---

## Career Levels

Every Career has **four levels**, each progressively more advanced than the last. New characters always begin at **level 1** of their chosen Career.

> **Example — Apothecary Career Levels:**
> 1. Apprentice Apothecary
> 2. Apothecary
> 3. Master Apothecary
> 4. Apothecary-General

---

## Advancing Through Your Career

Every Career offers three types of Advances, all purchased with **Experience Points (XP)**: Characteristic Advances, Skill Advances, and Talent Advances.

---

### Characteristic Advances

Each Career has an **Advance Scheme** covering its 10 Characteristics. Three are marked as available from **level 1**; the fourth unlocks at **level 2**, the fifth at **level 3**, and the sixth at **level 4**.

Each Characteristic Advance costs XP based on how many Advances you have already taken in that Characteristic (see the XP Costs table below). Each Advance adds **+1** to the associated Characteristic.

> **Example:** With an initial Agility of 27 and 4 Agility Advances, your current Agility is 31. Each of those 4 Advances cost 25 XP (0–5 Advances range).

> **Note:** Although **[Weapon Skill](/codex/characteristics/weapon-skill)** (**WS**) and **[Ballistic Skill](/codex/characteristics/ballistic-skill)** (**BS**) have "Skill" in their names, this is a historical artefact — they are **Characteristics** and are advanced as such.

There is no upper limit on Characteristic Advances, though higher levels become prohibitively expensive.

---

### Skill Advances

You may advance any **Skill listed at your current Career level or lower**. So a Master Apothecary (level 3) may advance Skills from levels 1, 2, and 3 of the Apothecary Career.

Each Skill Advance adds **+1** to your Skill level. Skill Advance costs are based on how many Advances you have already taken in that Skill (see the XP Costs table below).

> **Example:** With 9 Advances in **[Stealth](/codex/skills/stealth)** and an Agility of 31, your Stealth is 40. The first 5 Advances cost 10 XP each; the next 4 cost 15 XP each.

> **Note:** One Skill in the **first level** of your Career is marked in *italics* — this is your **Earning Skill**, used when earning money during play.

There is no upper limit on Skill Advances.

---

### Non-Career Advances

Characters may advance Characteristics and Skills not listed in their current Career — if the GM agrees. However, **non-Career Advances cost double** the XP listed in the table below. The GM may require you to find a teacher or otherwise justify this unusual training.

Non-Career **Talents** cannot normally be purchased with XP, but the **[Training](/codex/endeavours/training)** and **[Unusual Learning](/codex/endeavours/unusual-learning)** Endeavours provide opportunities to acquire them.

---

### Talent Advances

Talents are **only available at the Career level that lists them**. A 4th-level character cannot retroactively purchase Talents from levels 1–3.

Talent Advances cost **100 XP + 100 XP per time the Talent is already taken**:
- First purchase: 100 XP (unlocks the Talent's special rules).
- Second purchase: 200 XP.
- Third purchase: 300 XP.
- And so on.

> **Note:** Not all Talents can be purchased multiple times. Check the individual Talent listing for limitations.

### Talent and Career Change XP Costs

| Improvement | XP Cost |
|---|---|
| +1 Talent Advance | 100 XP + 100 XP per time already taken |
| Leave a Completed Career | 100 XP |
| Leave an Incomplete Career | 200 XP |
| Enter a Career from a different Class | +100 XP additional |

## Changing Career

When you have taken all the Advances you want in your current Career, it is time to change. Changing Career means one of two things:

1. **Moving to a different level** within your current Career (e.g. Apothecary → Master Apothecary).
2. **Moving to an entirely new Career** (e.g. Apothecary → Scholar).

In each case, first determine whether you have **completed** your current Career level.

---

### Completing a Career

Completing a Career means mastering your current vocation and preparing to move on. To complete a Career level, you must have:
- The required number of Advances (see table below) in **all Career level Characteristics**.
- The required number of Advances in **eight of your Career level's available Skills**.
- **At least 1 Talent** from your current Career level.

Advances gained before entering the current Career level count toward these requirements.

| Career Level | Advances Required |
|---|---|
| 1 | 5 |
| 2 | 10 |
| 3 | 15 |
| 4 | 20 |

> **Note:** Completing a Career level does **not** obligate you to move on. Staying at level 1 of Pauper indefinitely is perfectly valid.

---

### Changing to a New Level

If you have completed your current Career level, you may enter the **next level**, or any **lower level** of the same Career, for **100 XP**.

With GM permission, Career levels may be **skipped** when driven by in-game events.

> **Example:** A Scion (Noble level 1) inherits a significant estate during play. The GM offers Noble Lord (Noble level 4) as a Career change — still costs 100 XP if the current level is completed, 200 XP if not.

---

### Changing to a New Career

| Situation | Cost |
|---|---|
| Completed current Career → first level of any Career in **same Class** | 100 XP |
| Incomplete current Career → first level of any Career in **same Class** | 200 XP |
| Any of the above → Career from a **different Class** | +100 XP additional |

With GM permission and solid in-game justification, a character who has completed their current level may also enter the **same Career level** in any Career within their Class.

> **Example:** A completed Master Apothecary (level 3) could move to Fellow (Scholar level 3) to represent taking a teaching position at Altdorf University — 100 XP.

> **Note:** Some Careers (such as Wizard) require foundational knowledge before advanced lore can be understood. Talents from earlier Career levels are **not** available when skipping into a higher level.

Career changes may also occur between adventures using the **[Change Career Endeavour](/codex/endeavours/changing-career)**.

---

### Alternative Career Changes

Sometimes play throws up unexpected opportunities — a local Baron hires the party as his Honour Guard, or a fugitive from the Watch finds themselves effectively an Outlaw. The GM may offer Career changes outside the normal Career Path at any time. This is encouraged, as tying Career choices to in-game events makes for richer characters.

> **Optional Rule — Customise Your Trappings:** The Trappings listed in each Career are guidelines only. Adapt them to fit your character concept — a Handgunner from Reikland's State Army built on the Soldier Career might swap standard trappings for powder and shot, for instance.
