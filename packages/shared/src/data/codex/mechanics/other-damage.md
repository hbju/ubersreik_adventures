## Other Damage

### Drowning and Suffocation

Hold breath for **Toughness Bonus × 10 seconds** without a Test when prepared. Unprepared: suffocation begins immediately. Each Round of suffocation: lose **1 Wound**. At 0 Wounds: gain [Unconscious](/codex/condition/condition_unconscious). Then die in **Toughness Bonus Rounds**.

### Exposure

Every **4 hours** in a difficult environment (every 2 in extreme) requires an **[Endurance](/codex/skills/endurance)** Test.

| Failure # | Cold Effect | Heat Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **–10 BS, Agility, Dexterity** | **–10 Intelligence, Willpower**; 1 [Fatigued](/codex/condition/condition_fatigued) |
| 2nd | **–10 all other Characteristics** | **–10% all other Characteristics**; 1 [Fatigued](/codex/condition/condition_fatigued) |
| 3rd+ | 1d10 Damage (ignores AP; min 1 Wound); [Unconscious](/codex/condition/condition_unconscious) at 0 Wounds | 1d10 Damage (ignores AP; min 1 Wound) |

Stripping heavy trappings cancels 1 failed Heat Test.

### Thirst and Starvation

Endurance Tests to resist cumulative **–10 difficulty per test**.

| Resource | Test Frequency | 1st Failure | Subsequent Failures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water | Daily | **–10 Intelligence, Willpower, Fellowship** | **–10 all Characteristics**; 1d10 Damage (ignores AP) |
| Food | Every 2 days | **–10 Strength, Toughness** | **–10 all other Characteristics**; 1d10 Damage (ignores AP) |

Characters without food or water **cannot heal Wounds or recover Fatigue naturally**.
