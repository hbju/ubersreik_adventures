## KASPAR

**Picture idea:** a hard-eyed gang lieutenant in a long coat, caught mid-sentence in a dim alley
doorway, warm lamplight behind him — search something like "dark fantasy enforcer alley doorway
backlit portrait."

### Player-facing note

Two days chasing a ghost and mending a fence, and you're not sure which cost you more.

The first went to Ingrid, walking every dock, tavern, and back room with Pieter where she might have
surfaced — and finding nothing but the shape of her absence. People remember her. Nobody knows where
she went after Jannik died close enough to touch.

The second day you spent on Otto instead. You'd earned the attention — the Cage, the fame, the way
people say your name a little more carefully now — and you spent the day making sure that fame didn't
look like ambition to him. You pitched him on leading a real move against the Lowhavens. He listened
the way he listens to everything: pleased, careful, giving nothing away for free.

This morning, Wellentag 13th, crossing out of the Dunkelfeucht toward the Red Moon, he caught up with
you alone — that easy, unhurried way he has that always feels accidental and never is.

He wants Felix Scite, former champion of the Tin Spur, dead. Quietly, tonight if you can manage it. 
The Lowhavens have him drugged and fighting in some warehouse out by the Hog Pens, rebuilding themselves 
on his name, and Otto would very much like that name to stop being useful to them before it is. 

**What you picked up around the gang, these two days:**
- *"Mercy's not paying for a head yet. She wants a name - whoever opened Bella's throat at the
  Cage. A crown for a witness, five for a certainty. Ask around in a week and see if the price hasn't
  changed."*
- *"Otto wasn't the only one caught flat-footed before the Cage went down. Someone tipped the
  Lowhavens close enough to matter. Nobody's betting on his name yet - just on how many teeth he's
  got left once somebody works it out."*
- *"There's a real draw fighting out past the pens these nights — somebody good, kept quiet, kept
  drugged. Whatever's left of Bella's crew, they're not going down without one more show."*

### GM only

The Otto scene above **is** the scene — do not restage it live at the table, it's already "played."
The Mercy rumor is dramatic irony: Kaspar does not know it's about him. The mole rumor foreshadows
Rolf without naming him. The third rumor is ambient underworld awareness of the Hog Pit, so Kaspar
isn't blindsided when Silvi's ask lands this session.

## PIETER

**Picture idea:** a fog-drenched graveyard at dusk, tilted headstones, a lone hooded figure kneeling
at a fresh grave in the middle distance — search "gothic cemetery mist mourner silhouette."

### Player-facing note

The first day went nowhere, same as it did for Kaspar — Ingrid simply isn't in any of the places
people who know her would look.

The second day, you took different work: a nervous little riverside chandler named **Wenzel
Ottenreich** cornered you at the Red Moon, wringing his hands about a deed. His father, a stubborn and
suspicious old man, never trusted a notary in his life and had the original property deed to the
family warehouse buried with him rather than filed anywhere official. Now someone — Wenzel doesn't
know who, only that they're patient and their offer keeps getting smaller — is pressing hard to force
a sale before he can produce it. He paid what little he could and begged you to get it back before
the week's out.

You went to Morr's Garden at dusk. Getting to the family plot meant getting past a groundskeeper who
took his job more seriously than the pay justified, and past someone else already crouched near the
grave with a shovel of their own — you never got a clean look at his face, but he ran rather than
talk, and you came away with bruised knuckles and a torn sleeve for the trouble. The deed was
exactly where the old man's paranoia said it would be. So was a fine set of plate bracers, buried
with him for reasons nobody left alive can explain — Wenzel told you to keep them; the paper was all
he ever wanted.

While you waited for full dark to move, you saw something you haven't been able to put down since: a
hooded figure kneeling at a newer grave, off among the fresher stones, laying something small on the
mound. Before you got close enough to be sure of anything, she heard you, or sensed you, and was gone
into the mist toward the Morgenseite road. You didn't get a look at her face. You didn't need to. The
way she moved is going to bother you for a while.

That evening, swinging by the Tin Spur to check the fight schedule before Eisfange, a Watch corporal
you'd only ever nodded to — Corporal Vielfrass — sat down like an old friend and talked fighting the
way some men talk about wine. Before you quite noticed how, he'd invited you to "a proper fight, the
kind the Herald never hears about," out past the Hog Pens. Tonight, if you're free, he said. He made
it sound like a favor.

**What you picked up along the way:**
- *"There's a plot in Morr's Garden the diggers won't touch twice. Fill it in the morning, find it
  hollow again by dusk. The man underneath used to collect taxes, so even the ground wants nothing
  to do with him."*
- *"They're saying it was the Grey Sorcerer who killed the boy they found near his tower — the one
  they're calling Heske's hero now. The priests won't accuse him outright. They just keep asking why
  a brave man dies quiet and a wizard won't open his door."*
- *"The Pious Cup's taken to serving tea to people who don't drink it. A young woman with a blade she
  doesn't bother hiding stands by the door, and the old women call her 'sister' though nobody knows
  her name. They pray for the boy who died by the tower, and go quiet the moment anyone asks who saw
  him die."*

### GM only

The fresh grave is Jannik's; the hooded figure was Ingrid, visiting out of guilt she hasn't
processed. Keep her identity unconfirmed to Pieter — this is a glimpse, not a reunion. The third
rumor is a soft pointer at the Pious Cup (where she's hiding) without naming her. Wenzel Ottenreich
and the "patient buyer" pressing him are a quiet, optional thread — the buyer is one of Werner "the
Eel" Follmer's fronts, consolidating riverside property; use it later only if you want to pull that
string, it doesn't need to pay off on its own.

## THUCYDION

**Picture idea:** an elegant, smiling Watch officer leaning against a fighting-pit rail, torchlight,
faint menace under the charm — search "charismatic corrupt officer arena torchlight portrait."

### Player-facing note

Two days, and most of them went to proving yourself twice over — once to Christoph Engel, once to the
sand of the Tin Spur.

Engel finally gave you the real audience you'd been angling for. He listened to everything — the
rats, the device, the warpstone none of you have quite the vocabulary for yet — and didn't flinch,
exactly, but something in his stillness told you he understands the shape of this danger better than
you do. He promised to think of something before you go back down. He did not promise it would be
enough.

The rest went to training and coin at the Spur, and to Klaus's friends finding new ways to bring up
how thoroughly he put you on your back. Gunnar shared his kill with you as if it cost him nothing at
all. Ekkehard remembered your name, which from an ogre is its own kind of compliment.

One evening, a Watch corporal you'd only ever nodded to before sat down uninvited and talked to you
like an old friend — Corporal Vielfrass, easy smile, easier manners. He talked about fighting the way
some men talk about wine, and before you quite noticed how, he'd invited you to "a proper fight, the
kind that doesn't make the Herald" — out past the Hog Pens, tonight if you're free. He made it sound
like a favor. Maybe it was.

**What you picked up around the Spur:**
- *"A serving girl swears Gestaltenstark casts no reflection. He has the mirrors covered before every
  bout and pays in coin fresh enough to still ring true. Denvater Kretschmer told her to stop
  repeating it — which is exactly what a man says when he wants a thing kept quiet."*
- *"Word's going around that one of the old champions dropped clean off the map after the Cage fell —
  some say the Lowhavens have him, drugged and fighting under someone else's name, out past the pens
  where the Watch doesn't much bother looking."*

### GM only

First rumor is the established Gestaltenstark/werewolf thread, unchanged. Second rumor is a soft,
arena-plausible lead-in to tonight's Hog Pit job so Thucydion isn't blindsided by Silvi's ask.

## LUDWIG ("Ryan von Mounir")

**Picture idea:** a candlelit astrologer's shop, star charts and an old leather-bound book on a
cluttered desk, an unfinished glass of wine — search "occult astrologer study candlelight aesthetic."

### Player-facing note

Two days chasing a story that kept sliding out of your hands.

You went to the Seven Veils for Manticore lore to dress up your next piece for the Circle, and
Friedrich gave you exactly the unhurried, charming audience he always does — candlelight, good wine,
better manners than the material deserved. He invited you back for a small gathering on Festag night,
"friends of the shop," and you went, because turning down free wine and better company has never once
been your strength.

The party was pleasant in a way that itched afterward — too many compliments landing a beat too late,
too many questions about your family, your ambitions, what you actually believe about the stars, the
Empire, and your own place in either. You didn't perform as well as you'd have liked. Friedrich
noticed. He was gracious about it anyway, which somehow felt worse than if he hadn't been.

He lent you a book before you left — old, handsomely bound, ostensibly about Manticores and the
southern mountains — and told you to take your time with it. You haven't opened it yet. Something
about its weight in your bag makes you want to read it somewhere private, not at the Red Moon with
the others around.

**What you picked up:**
- *"At the Seven Veils, they say Friedrich can read your marriage in the fog on a mirror. Three
  brides-to-be from the Morgenseite have already handed him their betrothal rings for safekeeping
  'until the stars turn favorable.' The stars, it seems, have expensive taste."*
- *"Von Dabernick's letters to Altdorf arrive faster than the ink dries on the last one — the Cage,
  the pamphlets, the goblins, all filed as proof Ubersreik can't govern itself. Isolde calls it a
  very long job application for the post of jailer."*

### GM only

The book's contents are genuine Manticore lore — unlike the verbal information Friedrich's associate
fed him the night before at the party, which was subtly wrong. If Ludwig actually spends the several
hours needed to read it, he'll notice the discrepancy himself. This is the Unblinking Eye quietly
testing him; he has no idea. Keep simmering — no forced reveal this session. Elsa's debrief is still
owed and still pending; don't surface it unless Ludwig seeks her out.

## SILAS

**Picture idea:** a warm, cluttered physician's study — brass instruments, glass vials, low lamplight,
a doctor's private curiosity in her eyes — search "Victorian apothecary study candlelight portrait."

### Player-facing note

Two quiet days, by your standards — which is its own kind of unease.

The Physicians' Guild finally looked at the gut wound properly. Doktor Theodosia Grat did the work
herself — quick, precise, better hands than you're used to trusting near a wound that isn't your own.
She asked more questions than the job required. You answered fewer than she asked. She didn't push,
which you noticed, and which you're still deciding how to feel about.

The rest of the time went to Cordelia, picking up what scraps of the apothecary trade she had patience
to teach between customers. It wasn't much — an afternoon, maybe two — but you left knowing three new
names for things that can kill a man slowly, which feels like exactly the kind of education you
should be collecting right now.

You haven't seen Rolf since the Cage. When you asked after him at a tavern the gang frequents, the
answer came half a second too slow, and nobody quite met your eyes.

**What you picked up:**
- *"The Physicians' Guild is offering a shilling for the name of any barber-surgeon stitching wounds
  inside the walls without the right papers. Apparently saving a life without the correct diploma is
  considered more dangerous than losing one properly."*
- *"Shallya's temple is short on bandages that should still be sitting in storage since before
  Magnustag. The priestesses blame the wounded for hoarding; the wounded blame rats. Personally,
  you'd start with whoever's selling clean rolls at the Fleshmarket after dark."*

### GM only

First rumor is a real, personal risk given Silas's own unlicensed practice — a live Sword of
Damocles, not just flavor. Second rumor is a deliberate breadcrumb toward the Hog Pit guide's
bandage-fence lead (see generate_hog_pit_rescue.mjs, §3.1) — if Silas raises it at the table, it can
shortcut the party straight to that lead. The Rolf aside plants unease without confirming anything;
Silas still doesn't know about the treason.
