The last session was a resounding success. While a few details could have been refined and/or better prepared, there were many memorable moments that contributed to the progression of the story.

We began, more or less directly, by laying out the plan for the evening. Franz was able to present all the enemies we would face, and he noted that we would need to find a way to inflict harm upon the Nehekharian specter.

I was able to draw them a fairly clear map of the tavern. They decided on everyone’s positioning: Pieter and Thucydion would be upstairs—Pieter in Franz’s room, facing the exit from the kitchen to the courtyard, to ensure no one tried to enter from that side. Downstairs, Kaspar would hide in one of the alcoves in the main hall, placing him in a better position to defend Franz; Ludwig would stay in the kitchen to light the fuse; and Silas would remain outside, disguised as a beggar, to keep watch, sound the alarm if danger arose, and—if necessary—bar the door from the outside. Franz would stay in the main hall, feigning prayer while awaiting the arrival of the enemy.

The group then split up: Thucydion, Pieter, and Kaspar headed off to run some quick errands, while Ludwig and Silas set about preparing the tavern’s defenses, ensuring everything would be ready for the arrival of their enemies. Thucydion promptly consulted Cordelia—he had thought of her immediately, though he expected she might refer him to one of her acquaintances—and she provided him with a pinch of silver powder, capable of harming the specter. Pieter went to The Seven Veils, where he spoke once more with the proprietor, Friedrich, asking if he had any information regarding ghosts. Friedrich found the request rather peculiar; he didn’t have such information on hand, but suggested that if Pieter were to attend an event he was hosting the following evening, he might be able to look into it and share what he found. This could serve as a side scenario, separate from the Poetry Circle to which Ludwig and Silas have been invited. As for Kaspar, he went to purchase some weaponry, acquiring a few throwing knives and swords for his companions.

Back at the Red Moon, they were able to continue their preparations—stashing weapons here and there—before heading to their respective posts. Prior to that, they managed to share a drink and a few words with Franz, strengthening their bonds a little in the process.

Overall, things went more or less as planned, with the exception that the PCs had placed a cooking pot in the fireplace, preventing Shrinq from using it as an entry point. Instead, he had to climb through the window of Franz’s room—taking a penalty to his Stealth check—though Pieter failed to hear him regardless. Meanwhile, Thikad arrived and began shouting outside. Everything unfolded more or less simultaneously: the specter appeared just as the dwarf burst into the tavern, creating instant chaos. However, the players were prepared for the specter—largely because I had allowed them to apply a "charge" to their weapons to use against it, in addition to using such charges on a barrel of gunpowder (albeit with less effectiveness). Kaspar, Franz, and Ludwig managed to dispatch the creature quickly, even before Thikad could cause any real damage. At the same time, Shrinq caught Pieter from behind, landing a nasty blow with his dagger. Pieter managed to keep the creature at bay until Thucydion arrived to join the fray, and together they swiftly took it down.

Carmello and his men seized the moment to arrive; three of them held the line against Pieter and Thucydion, while the other four rushed downstairs to attack Franz. Things grew rather difficult for the group at this point. Ludwig, having lit the fuse, dashed to Franz’s side, while Kaspar faced the dwarf alone. Both combatants brought each other to the very brink of death, but Kaspar ultimately struck Thikad down, ending that immediate threat. Franz and Ludwig, however—outnumbered four to one—struggled significantly to fend off their assailants. Franz took a beating, and Ludwig was quickly caught off guard and wounded. Eventually, Thucydion managed to break free from his opponents upstairs—leaving Pieter to contend with the two remaining foes (the third having already been dispatched)—and returned swiftly to provide reinforcements. As the fuse continued to burn, Ludwig bolted to safety. Kaspar managed to drive back one of the four men attacking Franz, who was by then on the verge of death. Kaspar, himself nearly at his limit, also managed to escape; meanwhile, Thucydion arrived and saved Franz in the nick of time by eliminating the last of the attackers—only one of whom managed to survive and flee. Upstairs, Pieter also managed to overwhelm his two opponents before climbing out the window into the courtyard to escape. In the final seconds, Silas arrived and helped Thucydion carry the unconscious Franz outside—just moments before the fuse reached the powder keg. Everything exploded, destroying the tavern and incinerating the remains of their various adversaries. The group managed to escape, though not without sustaining numerous injuries. For his part, Thucydion had the presence of mind to retrieve Franz’s sword. Now, they still need to find a way to get him out of the city.


And so, we conclude the session here. In the next one, we will play through the end of the evening, with Franz waking up from his injuries and needing to reach the northern docks, where trusted associates are waiting to help him get out of the city. The players will have to successfully evade guard patrols—a third fire has broken out in the city in less than a week, leaving the guards on high alert as they hunt for the mad arsonist presumably behind it all. Ottokar will likely be lurking about as well, hoping to land a scoop; he is determined to unravel the mystery of these fires, which has the whole city talking. If they manage to get Franz to his destination, they will receive their ten crowns, along with Franz’s sword—which is currently in Thucydion’s possession.

As for the rest, we will play through the beginning of Marktag. I expect things will likely continue until late afternoon—before Silas and Ludwig need to head to the Poetry Circle, and Pieter (if he chooses) to Friedrich’s soirée. In the meantime, I imagine the group will pursue their investigation into Ingrid, perhaps attempting to make contact with Ricker or Ursula. Furthermore, I need to prepare the information Silvi will provide to the party—including, as we discussed, the potential red herrings she might offer to prevent the Lowhavens’ time in Ubersreik from coming to an end. Let’s coordinate on exactly what information will be shared; the goal is for the players to feel that the city is a living, breathing entity that exists independently of them—a place where new information is constantly emerging. They should be kept informed about concrete events taking place, alongside any misleading details provided by Silvi.
