The last session was really enjoyable, even if relatively quiet. We started, as planned, with Thucydion finishing his tailing of the guard squad; he managed to track them to their warehouse in the docks north of the Teufel. He kept watch for a while before going to fetch his companions. They were clearly keen to launch an assault on the warehouse, so I brought Ursula in at that point to intervene and talk them out of it. It had more or less the desired effect, although the player controlling Thucydion found Ursula’s arguments a bit weak — he felt a Sigmarite Inquisitor should have been more calculating and all too happy to find pawns like them who were eager to be sacrificed, whereas I had tried to portray her more as a suspicious lone wolf. The players took turns keeping watch on the warehouse until dawn.

In the morning, they went to see Silvi, who handed over their payment. They also asked for more details about the city's underworld. I gave them a brief overview of the general situation in Ubersreik - reflecting the growing trust the "Crosses" are placing in the group - but the players complained that the information wasn't particularly interesting and had largely been covered in previous sessions, especially during the evening at the *Eperon d'Etain*. So, I told them Silvi would get back to them once she had gathered more compelling details about what was brewing both below and above ground - the kind of intel the Crosses can access thanks to their unique position, straddling the criminal underworld and the higher echelons of society as a Cult of Ranald. We’ll need to hash out the specifics, but I also think Silvi needs to be careful not to give the Baron too much of an advantage right now. The Lowhavens have taken a beating lately - what with Bella’s death and the recent attack on the *Fosse aux Cochons* - and the Baron is likely to go for the jugular and finish the halflings off if given the chance. Silvi would almost certainly prefer to see the two gangs continue to weaken each other rather than let the Baron seize sole control of the city. I was thinking of mixing genuine information with misleading details - the sort Ludwig would be quick to pass on to Elsa. This could include rumors about secret alliances, troop movements, or fabricated plots designed to sow confusion and stave off the Lowhavens' demise, all while maintaining the appearance of neutrality on Silvi’s part.

Meanwhile, showing little interest in the information Silvi had gathered, Pieter headed alone to the Temple of Sigmar. There, he met Jacob, who opened up to him after Pieter mentioned the attack on the Tower (I was a bit too kind to Piete's PC in that encounter : I should probably have him make some Charm rolls and stuff. Jacob was a bit too eager to open up). Jacob shared details about Ernst (his quarters in the temple's undercroft) and Ursula (her falling-out with Ricker following Magnustag). He was keen to learn more about Aldric’s death and Tobias’s disappearance. He described Ursula as having a scarred face — a description that made it clear to the PCs that she was the very same woman they had encountered the day before. As for Ernst, the PCs are considering using the sewers to gain direct access to the undercroft.

The players then made a quick stop to see Wahlund to confirm their participation in the sewer expedition. He pledged that he, his three companions, and Melina would all take part. They also asked if any tunnels led directly into the undercroft of the Temple of Sigmar. Wahlund replied that there were indeed a few, but that they were heavily guarded and using them without taking precautions would be risky. The ball is now in the players' court, though I am far from certain whether they intend to go that way or simply tail Ernst to the Pious Cup.

As we wrapped up the last session, the PCs were arriving at Franz's place to start planning the evening...
