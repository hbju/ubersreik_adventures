La dernière session s'est très bien passée.

It began with the group meeting up at the Red Line, as usual, after spending two days resting and preparing for their next expedition into the sewers. There, they discussed their various options for what to do next; in particular, they were waiting for a sign from Silvi. As for Kaspar, following the mission Otto had entrusted to him, he immediately wanted to steer the group toward the Pig Pit, claiming to have heard rumors about Ingrid in that area.

As they continued their conversation, Franz seized the opportunity to approach the group and propose the task he had in mind for them (Red Moon Burning). The mission is as follows: during his time as a mercenary with the Grudge-Bearers, Franz made many enemies. He has learned through his sources that several of them have picked up his trail. Among those he knows are: Thikad Urgolsson (who swore the Slayer’s oath after losing his family heirloom—the runeblade *Grudge-Bearer*—in a game of Ranald’s Fingers that he believes Franz rigged); Shrinq Shaderipper (a Skaven assassin sent by the Augurs to eliminate Franz, who had been a constant thorn in their side during the Grudge-Bearers' Tilean campaigns); Carmello’s crew (a band of seven working for Marchesa Generosa Caramanici of the Border Princes; Lohner introduced the Grudge-Bearers to Lord Carl Svennson—Caramanici’s long-time rival—and will die a slow death for this crime); and Tah-Ra Mentuhr (a Nehekharan wraith awakened by the Grudge-Bearers during their campaign in Nehekhara; it seeks to possess Franz in order to infiltrate the mercenary group and assassinate their commander before he can thwart its master’s future plans. As a magical entity, this wraith can only be harmed by magical attacks).
Lohner’s plan is simple: lure all these unsavory characters to the inn the following day by announcing with great fanfare that he is closing the establishment to reflect and pray to Shallya on his birthday. Once as many enemies as possible are inside, he intends to blow up the inn, thereby faking his own death. However, he needs the group's help to acquire the gunpowder and detonate it, as well as to ensure everything goes according to plan and no one escapes. Once the deed is done, he must slip out of the city unnoticed, and he is counting on the PCs to secure a safe escape route for him. All of this is set for the following day, and he offers to pay the PCs 10 Gold Crowns for the job. If all the enemies are eliminated, he will also give them the sword *Saif Al-Janub*, which was awarded to him for his service during the defense of Sudenburg in 2498 IC. His only lead regarding the gunpowder is to check with Luigi & Salvatore. For now, Lohner hasn't briefed them on the specific enemies he has identified, but he has promised to provide more details the next day, prior to the mission.

The players told him they would consider her proposal, and Franz informed them that Silvi had summoned them. Pieter went to the Garden of Morr to see if he could find a note left by Ingrid or anything else there, but he found nothing. The rest of the group headed straight to the Twisted Hammer to speak with Silvi. The conversation went as expected: she explained that her hands were tied, but that she could potentially pay them handsomely for a job, thereby funding their expedition without arousing suspicion. She explained Félix's situation and asked for their help in finding him. Not all the PCs appreciated what they perceived as a lack of initiative on the part of the leader of the Crosses. Ludwig, in particular, lost his temper and accused Silvi of cowardice, feeling that she clearly recognized the threat yet was content to sit idly by and take no action. Tensions rose, and Silvi asked for Ludwig to be removed. The PCs then left, though not without accepting Silvi's mission (she had promised 20 Gold Crowns for the job, though I will likely raise that amount to 30).

The players first went to see Luigi & Salvatore, where they were informed that gunpowder had been rationed following the theft of large quantities a few months earlier (stolen by K&H and the Skaven when the forces from Altdorf captured the city). They were offered the chance to buy some at a much higher price than expected, or to try the "Axe and Hammer"—the main tavern for the Dwarfs of the Dawihafen—where supplies were apparently also available. One of the PCs noticed a bystander listening in, though they didn't pay it much mind; it was a member of Carmello’s gang, who took note of the PCs' presence. It will be important to have the players make a Perception check, as this individual might well follow them and discover their plan.

They then went together to the Fosse aux Cochons—a place Kaspar had been insisting on visiting since that very morning (without having shared the mission Otto had entrusted to him with the others), while Thucydion and Pieter (who had returned from the Garden of Morr) were both intrigued by Vielfrass's suggestion. Upon arriving and circling the neighborhood a few times, they spotted a suspicious-looking warehouse that seemed to match the descriptions they had heard. Since it was still early afternoon, they decided to head to the Dawihafen, whereas Kaspar chose to stay behind to scout the area. He was able to observe everything possible about the location—including the low chute—with a +4 DR bonus.

The others headed to the Dawihafen, but Thucydion—recognized by the dwarves from an incident during his previous visit—found his path blocked. Consequently, only Ludwig, Silas, and Pieter could proceed. They went to the *Axe and Hammer* to ask for gunpowder. Although the dwarves confirmed they had some, they demanded to know the reason for the request; the PCs (Ludwig in particular) found themselves lying—though perhaps not very convincingly—claiming it was for a fireworks display at Isolde Blucher’s home. This struck the dwarves as highly suspicious; they wondered what business the group could possibly have with the garrison captain's wife. Forced to spin even more elaborate lies, Ludwig claimed the powder was needed to eliminate a competitor: the rival *Red Moon* tavern. Unfortunately for them, the dwarves have a close relationship with Franz, and don't hold him as rival at all, so they summoned Grodni Surehammer — the tavern’s owner and the *de facto* leader of the Dawihafen. He demanded further explanations, and the situation began to escalate once again. The PCs were eventually arrested by the dwarves—after Pieter made an unsuccessful attempt to flee—and thrown into the *Axe and Hammer’s* cellars. They were interrogated for several hours until Franz himself arrived to defuse the situation. Grodni offered to let them leave with the gunpowder in exchange for a major favor they would have to perform for him later. As the session ended, Ludwig seemed reluctant to agree to this (though he will likely accept it next session, given that he has little other choice).
