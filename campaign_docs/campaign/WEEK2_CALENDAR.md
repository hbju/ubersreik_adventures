## Countdown pressure levers (use as needed)
- Wendt/Pfeffer: each day her file grows; a PC approach WITH EVIDENCE now lands differently than in S11.
- The escaped Clawleader: the Skaven know they're being hunted; expedition difficulty scales with delay.
- Silvi's proof: DELIVERED but **pushback has landed** — Temple, Council, and dwarves all blocking her. She can't act openly; the Felix rescue is indirect funding. Who's coordinating the pressure (Emmanuelle Nacht?) stays hidden.
- Wahlund's debrief: happened, but Wahlund kept his true Stromfels faith hidden behind a Manann cover story — the players still lack full vocabulary (warpstone, Rasknitt's true nature) to read the map they saw.
- Giordano's 20 GC: buys Skaven tactical lore + the Bastards' swords for the finale.
- **New — Red Moon Burning**: Franz's mission adds urgency AND a gunpowder thread. Carmello's crew watching the PCs is a ticking clock for the 14th's surprise factor.
- **Kaspar's deadline — 16th**: Felix rescued; Kaspar tried to kill him at the Crooked Hammer, but fellow PCs and Crosses intervened. No material immediate alliance damage. Otto's knowledge unconfirmed; Kaspar must handle his failed order before Felix's public return.
- **Fire aftermath (canonized)**: the Hog Pit and three neighboring dwellings were lost or condemned; about fifteen households are displaced; four civilians are confirmed dead and two remain missing. The Good Wife shelters survivors. Ottokar is collecting testimony, but nobody has publicly identified Kaspar as the fire-setter.
- **Next opening (S22)**: Backertag 16th c. 19:00, outside the Barracks. 0 GC; Thucydion 1/23 with no Fate. Tin Spur fights under way, Ursula waiting at the Chandlery, Elsa's summons pending.
- **Allies lost (S21)**: Kretschmer (named to the Watch) and Silvi (insulted by Pieter) are both gone. Silvi may relent only if they come back humbled and Pieter fights. Giordano's 20 GC is out of reach.
- **Reikhardt's charges**: none yet; pressed if Pieter does not fight. Trial at least a week away.
- **Red Moon aftermath**: third fire in under a week (Heske's shop, the Hog Pit, the Red Moon). City-centre blast: heavy damage, some civilian victims; crowds and the Watch are fighting the fires. The fled Carmello goon goes to ground: long-term risk to the false death.
