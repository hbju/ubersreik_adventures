## 1. THE CENTRAL PLOT — Kürbis & Honighäschen (K&H)

Two masked bandits terrorizing Ubersreik. **Truth**: they are Lotte and Gerhard, sole survivors of the Ludendorf Carnival massacre perpetrated years ago by a young Captain Andrea Pfeffer's drunken regiment in the Grey Mountains. Pfeffer doesn't know survivors exist. Now that she is Watch Captain of Ubersreik, they want revenge.

**Their plan**: Skaven allies (under a Warlock Engineer, part of Grey Seer Rasknitt's network) are digging tunnels to the Watch Barracks foundations and infusing the stolen Magnustag fireworks/blackpowder with warpstone. On Sigmartag 18th, while Pfeffer is at a public ceremony, they detonate the Barracks.

**Secret**: the Baron is quietly backing K&H (supplying grain via Werner → Günther → dead drop). Almost nobody knows this, including K&H's other contacts.

**Assets**: Sergeant Rudi Klumpenklug (blackmailed inside man in the Watch), Günther (unwitting supply runner from the Rat Catchers' Guild), Skaven allies, stolen blackpowder.

**What the PCs know**: Rudi is the inside man; dead drop at Lokhid's Depot (loose brick, south side); highly intelligent ratmen ("Skavens" — named by Giordano) allied with K&H; Pfeffer is the likely target; they have seen the lair, the Warlock Engineer, a device, and a map with highlighted points (couldn't read it). **S20: Engel named warpstone and its blast potential; a Watch crier announced Pfeffer's review in front of the Barracks on Sigmartag** (whether the PCs connected it is unknown). **S21: Pfeffer confirmed to them that Rudi keeps shady contacts; Thucydion heard a faint noise under the Barracks gaol at night without understanding it.** They do NOT know: the Barracks bombing plan (explicitly), K&H's identities/motive, the Baron's backing.

**What Pfeffer knows**: dismissed the PCs' warnings as conspiracy theories (jailed Ludwig & Silas briefly — freed by a Baron-paid guard), BUT quietly assigned Corporal Dietrich Wendt to tail Rudi. Wendt observed Rudi's nervous dockside meeting with an unidentified contact (it was Günther). By the 13th she has a small file. Skeptical but watching. **S21: visited the PCs in the cells on the night of the 15th; confirmed Rudi's shady contacts to them, mocked the rat-men and the werewolf story. They told her of corruption in the Watch, notably Vielfrass (to confirm at the table) — nothing about the Barracks, the review or warpstone.**

**K&H recon done**: Kürbis observed the Barracks and Pfeffer's ceremony movements during Magnustag.
