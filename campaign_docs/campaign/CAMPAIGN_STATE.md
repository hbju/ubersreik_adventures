## 1. THE CENTRAL PLOT — Kürbis & Honighäschen (K&H)

Two masked bandits terrorizing Ubersreik. **Truth**: they are Lotte and Gerhard, sole survivors of the Ludendorf Carnival massacre perpetrated years ago by a young Captain Andrea Pfeffer's drunken regiment in the Grey Mountains. Pfeffer doesn't know survivors exist. Now that she is Watch Captain of Ubersreik, they want revenge.

**Their plan**: Skaven allies (under a Warlock Engineer, part of Grey Seer Rasknitt's network) are digging tunnels to the Watch Barracks foundations and infusing the stolen Magnustag fireworks/blackpowder with warpstone. On Sigmartag 18th, while Pfeffer is at a public ceremony, they detonate the Barracks.

**Secret**: the Baron is quietly backing K&H (supplying grain via Werner → Günther → dead drop). Almost nobody knows this, including K&H's other contacts.

**Assets**: Sergeant Rudi Klumpenklug (blackmailed inside man in the Watch), Günther (unwitting supply runner from the Rat Catchers' Guild), Skaven allies, stolen blackpowder.

**What the PCs know**: Rudi is the inside man; dead drop at Lokhid's Depot (loose brick, south side); highly intelligent ratmen ("Skavens" — named by Giordano) allied with K&H; Pfeffer is the likely target; they have seen the lair, the Warlock Engineer, a device, and a map with highlighted points (couldn't read it). They do NOT know: the Barracks bombing plan, the warpstone, K&H's identities/motive, the Baron's backing.

**What Pfeffer knows**: dismissed the PCs' warnings as conspiracy theories (jailed Ludwig & Silas briefly — freed by a Baron-paid guard), BUT quietly assigned Corporal Dietrich Wendt to tail Rudi. Wendt observed Rudi's nervous dockside meeting with an unidentified contact (it was Günther). By the 13th she has a small file. Skeptical but watching.

**K&H recon done**: Kürbis observed the Barracks and Pfeffer's ceremony movements during Magnustag.

## 2. SESSION-BY-SESSION

- **Session 0: If Looks Could Kill**: The first session was a one-shot with a first set of PCs, of which only Kaspar and Pieter were kept afterwards. The players were hired to help a merchant, Rutger Reuter, to help with the construction of a mill near the village of Grausee, outside of Ubersreik. There, they were able to see that things are going poorly between Rutger and his business partner, a woman named Johanna Stiegler - the former lover of Kaspar. The PCs helped for a few days, until Rutger was found dead. They followed the trail to the Ortschlamm, were they defeated an ancient basilisk. Coming back to the camp, they found that the Striganys that had been hired had seen the death of Rutger as a bad omen, and left the camp behind. Thulgrim, the foreman, had stolen Johanna's money and fled. The session ended with the PCs deciding to go back to Ubersreik, and try to find out what happened to Johanna's money.

- **Session 1: The Red Moon Heist**: As Kaspar and Pieter went to the Red Moon Inn to celebrate their earned money from the first session, the masked bandits Kürbis and Honighäschen robbed the place. They met up on the spot with the others, Ludwig and Silas (who Kaspar knew already from the Gang), as well as three bounty hunters, Thucydion, Ingrid and Jannik. They all agreed to hunt the bandits to get their bounty (190 GC). The next day, when shopping by Cordelia's shop, the apothecary hired them to get rid of bounty hunters (Jannik and Ingrid, who were using her as bait to find the trace of Witch Hunter Hannah Baumann for another bounty). Thucydion followed her as she visited her friend Alexander, only to find Baumann trying to execute them both on the spot. Thucydion wounded Hannah, who fled. The PCs brought Alexander to Cordelia's Apothecary for healing.

- **Session 2: Threads and Tensions**: The PCs learned Cordelia was once a Jade College apprentice. Otto Gault warned them that chasing K&H was 'off limits' but hinted he'd look the other way for the right price. They dealt with Thulgrim, a dwarf thief that stole from Johanna Stiegler, the childhood sweetheart of Kaspar, and learned his tragic backstory.

- **Session 3: The Cold Trail**: The PCs tried to track Hannah but the trail went cold. Two weeks of downtime followed, as the PCs agreed to go their separate ways to gather information and resources. They all returned to Ubersreik on the 8th of Sigmarzeit, and met at the Red Moon Inn. The PCs had the following downtime activities:

Jannik and Ingrid arrived with intel — they witnessed K&H attack the Watch convoy, steal fireworks, kill four Watchmen. Ingrid joined Lohner's mission; Jannik refused due to injuries. The PCs gathered supplies from the Rat Catchers (Günther lent equipment), entered the sewers, got lost multiple times, and stumbled into a Mexican standoff with Leif's Band (mercenaries hired to kill the Goblins).

- **Session 5: Smoke and Cinders**: The PCs attacked Leif's Band to help the Goblins. Thucydion revealed his elven nature to frighten the Goblins; Kaspar intimidated them into obedience. The Goblins reached the surface and caused chaos at the Aschaffenberg party. The PCs looted the mercenaries (taking Gaenefys' elven bow) and left a note saying they bore no ill will. They got lost leaving the sewers and camped outside the city. Tension erupted over the elven bow — Ingrid accused Thucydion of taking the most valuable item. At dawn, they returned to find Heske's shop burned. Franz rewarded them and revealed Rudi Klumpenklug as K&H's inside man.

Pieter met Jannik (now a reluctant hero for saving Heske), Ingrid (proposed private Hannah hunt), and Ottokar Stiegler (newspaper publisher, Johanna's brother — Pieter didn't realize the connection).
