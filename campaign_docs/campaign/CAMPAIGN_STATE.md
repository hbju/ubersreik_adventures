## 1. THE CENTRAL PLOT — Kürbis & Honighäschen (K&H)

Two masked bandits terrorizing Ubersreik. **Truth**: they are Lotte and Gerhard, sole survivors of the Ludendorf Carnival massacre perpetrated years ago by a young Captain Andrea Pfeffer's drunken regiment in the Grey Mountains. Pfeffer doesn't know survivors exist. Now that she is Watch Captain of Ubersreik, they want revenge.

**Their plan**: Skaven allies (under a Warlock Engineer, part of Grey Seer Rasknitt's network) are digging tunnels to the Watch Barracks foundations and infusing the stolen Magnustag fireworks/blackpowder with warpstone. On Sigmartag 18th, while Pfeffer is at a public ceremony, they detonate the Barracks.

**Secret**: the Baron is quietly backing K&H (supplying grain via Werner → Günther → dead drop). Almost nobody knows this, including K&H's other contacts.

**Assets**: Sergeant Rudi Klumpenklug (blackmailed inside man in the Watch), Günther (unwitting supply runner from the Rat Catchers' Guild), Skaven allies, stolen blackpowder.

**What the PCs know**: Rudi is the inside man; dead drop at Lokhid's Depot (loose brick, south side); highly intelligent ratmen ("Skavens" — named by Giordano) allied with K&H; Pfeffer is the likely target; they have seen the lair, the Warlock Engineer, a device, and a map with highlighted points (couldn't read it). They do NOT know: the Barracks bombing plan, the warpstone, K&H's identities/motive, the Baron's backing.

**What Pfeffer knows**: dismissed the PCs' warnings as conspiracy theories (jailed Ludwig & Silas briefly — freed by a Baron-paid guard), BUT quietly assigned Corporal Dietrich Wendt to tail Rudi. Wendt observed Rudi's nervous dockside meeting with an unidentified contact (it was Günther). By the 13th she has a small file. Skeptical but watching.

**K&H recon done**: Kürbis observed the Barracks and Pfeffer's ceremony movements during Magnustag.

## 2. SESSION-BY-SESSION

- **Session 0: If Looks Could Kill**: The first session was a one-shot with a first set of PCs, of which only Kaspar and Pieter were kept afterwards. The players were hired to help a merchant, Rutger Reuter, to help with the construction of a mill near the village of Grausee, outside of Ubersreik. There, they were able to see that things are going poorly between Rutger and his business partner, a woman named Johanna Stiegler - the former lover of Kaspar. The PCs helped for a few days, until Rutger was found dead. They followed the trail to the Ortschlamm, were they defeated an ancient basilisk. Coming back to the camp, they found that the Striganys that had been hired had seen the death of Rutger as a bad omen, and left the camp behind. Thulgrim, the foreman, had stolen Johanna's money and fled. The session ended with the PCs deciding to go back to Ubersreik, and try to find out what happened to Johanna's money.

- **Session 1: The Red Moon Heist**: As Kaspar and Pieter went to the Red Moon Inn to celebrate their earned money from the first session, the masked bandits Kürbis and Honighäschen robbed the place. They met up on the spot with the others, Ludwig and Silas (who Kaspar knew already from the Gang), as well as three bounty hunters, Thucydion, Ingrid and Jannik. They all agreed to hunt the bandits to get their bounty (190 GC). The next day, when shopping by Cordelia's shop, the apothecary hired them to get rid of bounty hunters (Jannik and Ingrid, who were using her as bait to find the trace of Witch Hunter Hannah Baumann for another bounty). Thucydion followed her as she visited her friend Alexander, only to find Baumann trying to execute them both on the spot. Thucydion wounded Hannah, who fled. The PCs brought Alexander to Cordelia's Apothecary for healing.

- **Session 2: Threads and Tensions**: The PCs learned Cordelia was once a Jade College apprentice. Otto Gault warned them that chasing K&H was 'off limits' but hinted he'd look the other way for the right price. They dealt with Thulgrim, a dwarf thief that stole from Johanna Stiegler, the childhood sweetheart of Kaspar, and learned his tragic backstory.

- **Session 3: The Cold Trail**: The PCs tried to track Hannah but the trail went cold. Two weeks of downtime followed, as the PCs agreed to go their separate ways to gather information and resources. They all returned to Ubersreik on the 8th of Sigmarzeit, and met at the Red Moon Inn. The PCs had the following downtime activities:
    - Silas made contacts among rat catchers (Günther)
    - Thucydion beat up dozens of Dockers (now hunt a 'ghost')
    - Kaspar dealt with gang tensions
    - Pieter became accidental riot figurehead, heard about Ulliafor Sunbright to learn how to be more charismatic. 
    - Ludwig created 'Ryan von Mounir' identity, got Poetry Circle invite

- **Session 4: Whispers and Smoke**: Franz Lohner proposed a deal: help Goblins crash an Aschaffenberg party in exchange for info about K&H. Jannik and Ingrid arrived with intel — they witnessed K&H attack the Watch convoy, steal fireworks, kill four Watchmen. Ingrid joined Lohner's mission; Jannik refused due to injuries. The PCs gathered supplies from the Rat Catchers (Günther lent equipment), entered the sewers, got lost multiple times, and stumbled into a Mexican standoff with Leif's Band (mercenaries hired to kill the Goblins).

- **Session 5: Smoke and Cinders**: The PCs attacked Leif's Band to help the Goblins. Thucydion revealed his elven nature to frighten the Goblins; Kaspar intimidated them into obedience. The Goblins reached the surface and caused chaos at the Aschaffenberg party. The PCs looted the mercenaries (taking Gaenefys' elven bow) and left a note saying they bore no ill will. They got lost leaving the sewers and camped outside the city. Tension erupted over the elven bow — Ingrid accused Thucydion of taking the most valuable item. At dawn, they returned to find Heske's shop burned. Franz rewarded them and revealed Rudi Klumpenklug as K&H's inside man.

- **Session 6: Masks and Shadows**: Split party session. Pieter met Jannik (now a reluctant hero for saving Heske), Ingrid (proposed private Hannah hunt), and Ottokar Stiegler (newspaper publisher, Johanna's brother — Pieter didn't realize the connection). Thucydion visited Cordelia, who warned about potential zealot targets. Kaspar spotted Rudi but didn't follow him. The trio tried to warn potential targets — got thrown out by dwarfs, found Old Mathilde missing (River Troll victim, not zealots), discovered Emilia Lochland (hedge witch at Bridge House Inn) was one of the mercenaries they fought. Giordano proposed settling the grudge at the Tin Spur.
Ludwig and Silas attended the Poetry Circle. Ludwig recited anti-elf poetry interpreted as criticism of the ruling class. When the inner circle withdrew for their private meeting, Ludwig and Silas left abruptly without saying goodbye — a servant confronted them.
At the Crooked Hammer, Ingrid and Pieter played Cerevis (drinking game) with Rudi and Hieronymus (Ludwig's nemesis, the gambler who ruined him). They got blackout drunk. Ludwig and Silas arrived late, took over, got drunk themselves. Hieronymus suggested visiting Kat House (brothel where Ludwig's ex Vespère works). As they tried to leave with Rudi, Silvi Kreutzhame (Ranaldan priestess, tavern owner) intervened. Fight broke out.

- **Session 7: Debts and Dues**: The fight continued. Silvi cast Blessing of Conscience on Thucydion, then Perfect Empathy — learning his shame as a failed Faniour. The PCs were eventually defeated. Under Silvi's supervision, they interrogated Rudi, learning about the dead drop near Lokhid's Depot. They set up surveillance at the dead drop. Someone retrieved the message; Thucydion tailed him into a warehouse where he retrieved a large sack. He then followed him until the Rat-Catchers' Guild.
Ludwig and Silas were summoned by Elsa 'The Stitch' Gruber. The gang leader had learnt how few the duo had done for the Baron despite the clear orders they have, and the ruckus caused at the Crooked Hammer. She ordored Silas to remove a tooth from Ludwig. Silas bargained to brand Ludwig instead. Ludwig now bears the Baron's mark and is ordered to return to the Poetry Circle on Marktag 15th.

- **Session 8: Searching for the Man**: Backertag 8th Sigmarzeit. 
Thucydion felt someone tailing him while getting back to the rest of the group. Unbeknownst to him, Gaenefys is stalking him, hoping to get back her bow in due time. While traveling in the city, Kaspar bumped into Otto, who told him about the Baron's plans to seize the Gilded Cage on Magnustag. Otto explicitly asked him to bring his cousin Pieter along with him - but Kaspar seems clearly reluctant on doing so. On the other hand, he told Silas and Ludwig to come as well.
They then all met back in the Red Moon Inn. There, they gathered everything they learned - mainly Thucydion telling them about the mysterious man and the Rat Catchers Guild. They investigated some leads for the whole afternoon, not finding anything conclusive. 
At the end of the day, as the rest of the group found their way back to the Red Moon to share their empty-handed research, Pieter and Ingrid, staying at the entrance of the sewers outside of the city, saw something, swimming, on the Teufel, coming towards them from the other bank. A strange Beastman, rat-faced, got out of the water and entered the sewers. Not willing to lose this trail, Pieter followed it discreetly, until a junction where the Ratman got the message and the sack from the mysterious man, hidden. But at this moment, it noticed Pieter and attacked it : a brief skirmish engaged, from which Pieter quickly came out on top. The creature managed to escape, but Pieter could see it going back to the other bank.

- **Session 9: Into the Dark**: As Pieter got back to the Red Moon Inn via the sewers, the rest of the group quickly decided on following the trail of the mysterious creature. They got out of the city, got a barge on one of the neighbouring village, and quickly arrived on the other side of the Sigmarsewers exit, only to find a similar entrance to the northern, dangerous Magnussewers. There, they decided on exploring it. They got some random encounters, including fighting a group of giant rats, trying to sneak by a sleeping troll, only for Kaspar to attack it, risking the whole mission. They managed to defeat it, but only after Kaspar and Pieter got hit pretty badly. After having slain the beast, Kaspar found a rotting corpse, half eaten, with numerous charms and trinkets, that matched the description of Old Mathilde. The river troll had the habit of going out of the sewers, lurking in the Teufel to snatch some poor folk who wandered too long in the Dunkelfeucht, and eat them back in his home, in the sewers. However, the party doesn't know that, and still thinks something fould might be at work here. 
They then continued trailing the rat, and finally found the lair, where they witnessed the warplock engineer working on a strange device, two rat ogres, and a map with different highlighted points. Thucydion, fearing to be discovered, quickly convinced the rest of the group to flee.They got out of the sewers, in the middle of the night, and got back to the Red Moon Inn.
The next day, Silas and Ludwig quickly visited the Rat Catchers Guild, hoping to find Günther, only to meet Wahlund's group of three norsemen. They sold the rat tails they had found, and got on their way.
