Magnustag Approaches!

In but five days, the citizens of our fair town shall gather to honor Magnus the Pious with the traditional building of the 'Maggies.'

After two weeks of unrest and riots, is this really all the greedy Nobles can come up with to qualm the people's cause ?? The Herald shall be watching. Will YOU?

Wizard's Way Inferno Narrowly Averted!

Last night, a gang of arsonists attempted to burn the establishment of one ELARA, an Elf seamstress of dubious reputation. The blaze was prevented not by our vigilant Watch — oh no! — but by a band of common criminals who happened to be lurking nearby.

This editor has learned that mere weeks ago, another Elf was seen assaulting a respected Witch Hunter at Rugger's Boarding House. Are these incidents connected? Is there an Elf conspiracy afoot in Ubersreik? The long-lived ones keep their secrets close, but the Herald keeps closer watch!

Citizens are advised to report any suspicious Elven activity to the Watch. Or to this publication, if the Watch proves... indisposed.

The Ghost of the Docks!

A phantom stalks the honest laborers of our docks! Dozens of Dockers have been found beaten senseless over the past fortnight, with nary a witness to the attacks. The victims speak of shadows, of silence, of waking in gutters with broken bones and shattered pride.

The Dockers' Guild offers five gold crowns for information leading to the capture of this spectral menace. The Boatmen's Guild has doubled this bounty. Is it a daemon? A vengeful spirit? A very angry man with excellent footwork?

REWARD: 10 GC for Information!
Contact the Dockers' Guild!

Riots Damage Beloved Boutique

The recent civil unrest — which this publication attributes to foreign agitators and Jungfreud sympathizers — reached its shameful peak when rioters damaged the vitrine of Madame Beaumarteau's, the premiere costume establishment serving Morgenseite's finest families.

Thanks to the swift action of the Watch and the steadfast resolve of our Guild leaders, order was restored before further damage could occur. The Herald commends Burgomeister Maler and the Town Council for their measured response.

Madame Beaumarteau's remains OPEN FOR BUSINESS. Support local commerce!

EXCLUSIVE: Gang War Brewing?

Your intrepid editor has spent weeks investigating the criminal underworld of our beloved Ubersreik, and the findings are alarming. TWO criminal organizations now vie for control of the Dunkelfeucht and the Docks: the mysterious 'Baron' and the Halfling 'Mercy' of the Lowhaven Clan.

Shopkeepers speak in whispers of protection payments and territorial disputes. The Watch, stretched thin by... who knows what they are doing ?, seems powerless to intervene.

How long before this simmering conflict boils over into open warfare on our streets? The Herald demands answers! Future issues shall reveal MORE!
